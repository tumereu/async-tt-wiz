## 9.4 Known specification gaps

These are unfinished artifacts in their respective D-115 stages, not an assertion
that every item needs a new product decision or blocks completion of stage 1:

- **Contract/verification completion:** per-action field/precondition/cleanup
  mappings, worked complete-game/edge-case batches, grouping/recovery outcomes
  and traceable acceptance scenarios. Apply agreed rules before asking again.
- **Stage-3 UI:** annotated screens, copy, geometry, gesture arbitration and
  accessible alternatives, incorporating already-approved interactions.
- **Relevant technical/operational design:** storage/schema/API/migrations,
  parameter bounds, provider/hosting/rights, security/privacy and measurable
  performance/recovery objectives. Product promises still need explicit approval.

Keep explicit outcomes rather than vague "sensible defaults", but do not turn
their derivation or representation into an unnecessary owner questionnaire.
