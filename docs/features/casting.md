# Casting

Raising a hand card and its cast chips, the cast panel, choosing ways and modes, picking targets on the table, paying
and autotap, how a cast shows and when it is dropped.

## How it works

Your hand has no menu: a tap raises a card and shows chips above it, one for each way to cast or play it, with its
price, and a tap on a chip casts it that way at once. A second tap on the raised card, or a double tap, casts it at once
when there is nothing to choose and otherwise opens its cast panel above your hand. The panel takes the choices in rules
order (the way and extras, modes, X, the way to cast, the payment), and Cast on the main button sends the cast on.
Targets and choices among permanents are picked on the table itself, with a slim bar above your hand saying what is
being chosen. Autotap pays from your mana sources by default; while the panel is open the sources that pay are outlined
on the table, and you change them by tapping. Cards in other zones that you may use (flashback, unearth and the like)
sit at the end of your hand row and work like hand cards. An open cast is yours alone until Cast, and Back or Undo drops
it without a trace.

## Raising a hand card

- **ZON-079** Your hand has no menu: a tap raises your card a little (on the desktop table it pops out of the fan along its turn until whole in view, WEB-015). A card you can't use now isn't raised, only shaken; the other player's hand cards ignore taps. (r64)
- **ZON-101** A raised hand card outlines in amber the sources Autotap would tap for its regular cast, for its smallest cost only where it can have several (ZON-080; in a stack of identical sources its topmost ones, TBL-130; none for a land or a pool-paid cast; while a chip is held, that chip's, ZON-080). (r64)
- **ZON-102** A second tap on the raised card casts or plays it at once when that is all it can do now (its regular cast, a greyed way beside it being no choice, asking nothing before its payment); otherwise it opens the card's cast panel (STK-023, ZON-082) and never picks a chip, the chips flashing once over the panel as it opens, a hint that a chip casts directly. (r64)
- **ZON-103** Tapping elsewhere or another hand card lowers the raised card, as do casting it and its leaving the hand; board changes refresh its chips and outlines. It moves at hand-move speed. (r64)
- **ZON-004** Double-tapping a hand card casts it through Automatum (STK-023) as a second tap on the raised card does (ZON-102): at once when it has no choice, otherwise through its cast panel. A card Automatum doesn't allow now (any card in the other player's hand too) stays in the hand and shakes, saying why (TBL-212). (r78)
- **ZON-104** A land double-tapped in your hand is played at once onto its owner's battlefield, and a trigger it sets off (landfall) is passed on by its player as after a cast (STK-026). (r58)
- **ZON-095** While a cast (its target bar too), an Automatum choice of yours or another panel (a reveal, a Library, View deck, the Stack's list, the table menu) is open, your hand row is dimmed to about 40% and takes no taps, double taps or drags (nothing raises, casts or shakes), unless the open choice is about a card in your hand (a discard, a cost discarding or exiling a hand card); closing it lights the hand again. Never during your opening: the hand you decide a mulligan on shows at full strength (TRN-062). (r74)

## Cards you may use from other zones

- **ZON-105** Cards outside your hand that you may use from where they are show in your hand row (ZON-106): those a permission lets you cast or play from there (flashback or escape in your graveyard, a foretold card in exile, the top of your library under "you may play lands from the top of your library", …) and those with an ability or special action that works there (unearth, embalm, eternalize, scavenge, encore, "Return this card from your graveyard to your hand", …). (r64)
- **ZON-106** These cards show face up at the right end of your hand row after a thin divider, each with its zone's badge (the empty pile's glyph, white in a dark disc) in its top-right corner, while their pile still holds them; only cards you may see, and the other player's table shows none of yours in a hand row (ZON-136). They are hidden while you have no such use. (r64, owner 2026-11-22)
- **ZON-136** The other player's cards they may use from outside their hand (their hand row's, ZON-105) show as one round chip in their row of round chips right after their dungeon's room, edged in the playable cyan with "▶" and the count; a tap lists those cards drawn as small cards, each with its zone's pile badge under it, the one your own hand row cards from other zones carry (ZON-106; a zone without a pile shows its word, "Command zone"); holding one you can see inspects it, and one you can't is a card back, named only by where it is ("The top card of their library"). (r76, owner 2026-11-21)
- **ZON-107** Otherwise they behave exactly as hand cards: they glow while Automatum lets you do any of their uses now (TBL-117), a tap raises them with the chips a hand card shows (ZON-080: each way to cast or play them and each such ability, greyed when it can't be done now), they are double-tapped and dragged as hand cards, and they are dull and only shake when none of their uses can be done now (TBL-128). A way using the card's own permission is named by its keyword ("Flashback {R}"). (r64)

## Cast chips

- **ZON-080** Chips in a row above a raised hand card rise and fade with it, one per complete way to cast it (even if only one), white with a violet outline: the regular "Cast" ("Play" for a land) first, each way with its extras off first; every way shown whether or not it can be done now. (r64)
- **ZON-108** Each chip is a way of the card with its optional extras decided, named for it ("Cast", "Cast with offspring", "Cast with kicker and offspring"; another face or Adventure by its name, "Flashback", "Evoke", a split card's fused halves, its special actions "Plot", "Foretell" and its abilities used from the hand "Cycling" and its kinds, "Channel", "Ninjutsu"; for a card of your hand row from another zone, ZON-105, its abilities and special actions used from that zone, "Unearth", "Embalm"; a plain "cost: effect" one reading as on the card, its cost and then its effect). (r64)
- **ZON-109** A chip shows its way's whole mana cost (a long name wraps onto more rows, never cut; an X as {X}). A way that can have several costs (a cost depending on the target, a mode or the like, or a search that couldn't finish) shows the smallest legal one, the lowest mana value it can pay now and the first listed on a tie, then "+" and a generic mana symbol holding a question mark ("{R} + {?}", our own notation, not real MTG). (r64)
- **ZON-110** At most 4 chips: a card with more complete ways shows one chip "Cast…" with its cheapest cost (the cheapest it can pay now), which opens its cast panel with every choice (ZON-082). An extra that can be paid several times (multikicker) is paid at most once in the chips, more often in the cast panel. (r64)
- **ZON-111** A tap on a chip casts (or uses) the card that way at once, Autotap paying (STK-024); targets, modes and X are then asked as needed (STK-023). Holding a chip outlines the sources Autotap would tap for it and shows a card-like popup with its name, cost and printed text, casting nothing. (r64)
- **ZON-112** A chip Automatum shows can't be done now is greyed and does nothing (a land's "Play" too, while it can't be played). Chips show prices and never deny a card Automatum lists (TBL-117): a way it lists with no payment Autotap can find is a live chip, and the cast panel opens to pay by hand (STK-024). (r64)

## The cast panel

- **STK-023** Casting with a chip (ZON-080) or a menu row (TBL-126) opens the cast panel only for a real choice (modes, X, targets and the like); with only the payment left, the cast goes out at once, Autotap paying (STK-024), and when Autotap finds no way the panel opens for you to pay by hand. A tap on a raised hand card with any choice opens its panel (ZON-102) with its ways and extras (ZON-082), stopping before the payment until Cast. (r58)
- **STK-135** The cast panel is white, the table's full width, docked above your hand (over the Stack too) with a violet top edge, and has the card at its right (an ability as its token, STK-044). Its title says what is done and with which card ("Cast Storm Spark"; "Activate Ember Herald", by the ability's source, with the ability's text under it; "Play Mountain"); a long name wraps onto a second line and is then cut with …. (r58)
- **STK-136** Under the panel's title is the sentence the open choice asks about (an activation's text, a spell's sentence naming what is chosen: "Ember Burst deals X damage to any target."); there is none for modes, whose chips are their sentences, or for the payment. (r58)
- **STK-137** Then comes a section per choice in rules order: the way and extras of a hand card (ZON-082), modes (STK-040), X (STK-138), the way to cast (STK-051), and last what pays it (STK-024). Choices stay changeable, keeping later ones that still fit, without a spinner. (r58)
- **STK-138** X is set with − value +: − and + stop at its least and at the most your mana pays now, reckoned with the cheapest possible target since X comes before targets (CR 601.2b, 601.2c), so X is never a dead end and the targets then narrow by the X chosen. There is no OK: Cast answers it and goes on (STK-052). (r58)
- **STK-139** X's value sits in a box that keeps one width for up to 4 digits, and a tap on it opens a numeric input in its place for any value: a field with the number keyboard, a check that takes it and a cross that keeps the value, its range under the field, a value outside it refused with why and the check greyed. (r58)
- **ZON-082** A hand card's cast panel (ZON-079, the "Cast…" chip) shows its ways as chips under "Cast as" (the one cast filled violet; a greyed one does nothing) and its extras under "Extras" as amber-outlined chips with "+" before the name and a check box (kicker, buyback, offspring, gift and other optional additional costs): a tap turns an extra on or off and never casts; one that can be paid several times (multikicker) counts up per tap ("×N") with a small "−". (r58)
- **ZON-113** A hand card's cast panel opens on the first way you can pay with every extra off; changing a way or an extra keeps the choices made below it that still fit and the sources you picked that still pay (STK-024). With ways or extras to choose it opens before the targets: Cast goes on to them. (r58)
- **STK-051** In a cast begun from a card menu, when Automatum offers several ways to cast, the panel shows them as chips (faces, both halves of a fuse card as "Reel // Rend (fused)", which also names the spell, alternative costs), and optional costs ask "Pay the Kicker cost?" No/Yes, then show a switch. A hand card's ways and optional costs are its chips (ZON-080) or its panel's way and extra chips (ZON-082), never asked. (r58)
- **STK-040** Choosing modes: with one to choose a tap on a mode answers; otherwise taps choose and unchoose and OK sends once the count fits (in the cast panel Cast does, with no OK, STK-052). A mode that may be chosen again (a Season's pawprint modes) is added per tap ("×2"), with "−" taking one back and Clear taking all; pawprint modes show their cost ("{P}{P}") and "Pawprints: N of 5" the total, red with OK greyed while over the limit. Each mode chip's mark in front fills once chosen. (r54)
- **STK-052** The cast panel's bottom row has the eye at its left (STK-044) and no button: while the panel is open, Cast (Activate, Play) with its icon is the main button (TRN-063), doing what the panel's button did (a quick second tap is ignored there); there is no Cancel (Undo and Back drop the cast, STK-025). (r62)
- **STK-140** A choice built up in the cast panel (X, several modes, a division, cards to pick) has no OK of its own: Cast answers it and goes on (to the targets, or out), and its hint says "then Cast"; in a hand card's panel still choosing its way, Cast goes on to the targets (ZON-082). Holding cards to inspect them works throughout; while Automatum works out a step the panel takes no taps. (r62)
- **STK-141** Cast is greyed (holding it says why) only while a choice built up in the panel isn't complete or can't be paid (STK-047; an X too where a cost reduction that starts to apply as X grows leaves values that can't be paid, CR 601.2f: Cast greys on those, and once 20 values in a row can't be paid the stepper stays open up to its top, the payment's own check deciding) or the mana chosen doesn't pay (STK-024). (r62)

## Picking targets on the table

- **STK-047** Targets are picked on the table: the cast panel becomes a slim bar above your hand, with the card at the bar's right (STK-044), while every legal target glows violet (players by their life squares, spells and abilities only as Stack entries; under a finger a target's glow grows a little and brightens, TBL-117). Other taps shake (TBL-118). (r62)
- **STK-142** The target bar shows its title ("Choose any target") over the source's name (STK-226) and the whole text of the spell or ability whose targets or costs are picked, in small grey text, up to 4 lines, showing which pick is which: the phrase picked now in violet on a softly pulsing light violet ("Choose target player" over "… dies, target player loses 1 life …" with "target player" shining). (r62)
- **STK-143** In the target bar each phrase already chosen is replaced by what was chosen, named in its controller's colour and reading naturally for several or none ("Ash Pup and Ember Cub", "no creature"). A choice with no phrase of its own (a keyword's target: Equip's, support's) shines the keyword's name while it is picked and has what was chosen named after it in brackets ("Equip (Ash Pup)—Sacrifice a creature."). (r62)
- **STK-144** In the target bar a spell's additional or alternative cost comes as its own line before the spell's text while it is picked or once chosen. There is no summary to confirm. When the lines cut the sentence, a tap on it shows it whole, scrolling past half the table's height, and another tap goes back; a sentence that fits takes no taps. (r62)
- **STK-145** For several targets, how many are picked shows beside the title ("1 of 2", "2 picked", "1 of up to 3"), and Done, or None while nothing is picked, is the main button (TRN-063), greyed until what is picked can be finished. There is no Cancel: Undo and Back drop the cast (STK-025). (r62)
- **STK-146** A battlefield group holding a target opens on a tap, its members picked one tap each (TBL-130). With several targets, each one picked so far shows picked: its violet glow at full strength, filled violet with a white check in a violet dot at its top right, a card alone and an opened group's member alike (TBL-130). (r62)
- **STK-147** A Graveyard, Exile or other pile holding a card you may target or choose glows violet over its whole face, slowly pulsing with rings spreading from its edge, so it reads "tap me, your choices are inside"; tapping it opens its cards one by one, the legal ones glowing, until the targets are chosen. (r62)
- **STK-048** When a Stack entry below the top is a legal target, the Stack glows; tapping it opens the Stack in place as its list (STK-018) while the target bar stays above your hand: the legal entries glow violet and a tap on one picks it, the others are dimmed; holding an entry inspects it. Close or a tap outside the list closes it, back to picking on the table. (r54)
- **STK-148** Each card on the table picked so far in the cast or the trigger, the ones picked now included, carries a small violet chip just above its top left corner saying what it is for: "Target", a keyword's target by its keyword ("Equip"), a cost's by what is done to it ("Sacrificed", "Tapped", "Returned", "Exiled"). (r62)
- **STK-149** While you cast or activate you are offered only candidates legal given what you have picked so far (the cost a target makes, the creature sacrificed for emerge, the creature tapped for harmonize; targets that must share or differ in something): a candidate glows and can be picked when some finished selection including it can be paid and is legal, not only when the selection pays right now, since a cost can fall as you add targets, and the card counts as castable when one exists. (r62)
- **STK-150** Picking or unpicking works every candidate out again, off the main thread: the picked look is at once, the glows follow, and a search bound keeps a candidate selectable. Done (or None) is enabled only on a selection that really can be paid. (r62)

## Choosing permanents on the table

- **STK-151** Every cost or choice whose candidates are all permanents on the battlefield, whichever player answers it (a sacrifice or another cost choosing your permanents, such as creatures to tap, crew and saddle included, or a permanent to return or exile; an edict; "choose a creature" or "choose a creature an opponent controls" while resolving), is picked on the table the same way as targets (STK-047). (r62)
- **STK-152** Such a choice's slim bar is titled with the choice ("Sacrifice"; a sentence too long for a title says how many, "Choose one") over the sentence it asks about (a cost's over the whole text, its phrase shining as a target's does: "a creature" of "Sacrifice a creature"), with the count beside the title; the candidates glow violet, picks show picked, groups open on a tap, and Done or None is on the main button (greyed until the picks can be finished: crew's until their total power reaches it). (r62)
- **STK-153** A choice among cards in a hand, a library, a graveyard or mixed zones keeps its panel (STK-030); a payment while something resolves is made on the table too (STK-049). (r62)

## Paying and Autotap

- **STK-024** With "Autotap mana sources" on (LOB-113, the default) a cast that needs nothing else goes out paid with Automatum's suggestion without asking; with Autotap off nothing is chosen first and you pick every source. A spell with convoke (its own or granted) always opens the panel, also with Autotap on. Autotap is a convenience and never decides whether something can be cast (TBL-117): when a way seems to exist and it finds none, the panel opens for you to pay by hand. (r74)
- **STK-154** Autotap pays in this order: pool mana; sources with no side effects, the cheapest first (lands before creatures, single colours before many); sources that deal damage or take life; sacrifices and other things used up; a source whose damage would be lethal only when it is the only way to pay ("we have undo"); it never pays life you don't have. Within each of these tiers (they never trade off against each other) it pays the way that leaves the most of your spells and abilities still payable with the untapped sources left: the cards in your hand, your hand row's cards from other zones (ZON-105) and your permanents' activated abilities, whatever their timing, counting only whether the sources left can make each one's mana (a {1} paid with a Mountain rather than your only Island while you hold a blue card); ties keep the order above, and with too many ways to compare it keeps that order too. The same choice is the raised card's and held chip's outline (ZON-101, ZON-111), fills in around your own picks (STK-158) and pays while something resolves (STK-168). (r74, owner 2026-11-22)
- **STK-155** A cast that asks only targets still goes out at once after its last target; there is no source picking in the target bar: to pay another way tap and float the mana before casting, or turn Autotap off (the panel then opens after the targets). Tapping sources before casting still works: their mana goes to your pool (TBL-122) and pays first. (r74)
- **STK-156** While the cast panel is open (not its target bar) what pays the cast reads from the table: the sources chosen, Autotap's and yours alike, are outlined in amber there, each leaving its stack (TBL-053) for a pile of its own right of what is left of it, in the same row, so they are easy to see and unpick, and going back when taken out or when the panel closes. (r74)
- **STK-157** The panel's compact "Mana · {cost}" section says only "Outlined sources pay. Tap sources on the table to change them."; no source is named in words, not even one under the panel or panned away (the eye and panning show it). Your pool's mana is in that section too (STK-228); life, other ways the table can't tap, and sources in your hand are chips there. (r74, owner 2026-11-22)
- **STK-228** In a payment (the cast panel, a payment while something resolves) each mana of your pool is a small card showing its mana symbol in its colour ({R}, {G}, {C}), never a generic chip: a tap chooses it or takes it out, a chosen one is ringed in violet with a check, and holding it says what it is and anything it carries (the source that made it, a limit on what it may be spent on). The payment line names it by colour ("Paid with Red mana from your pool"). (owner 2026-11-22)
- **STK-158** Autotap's suggestion is chosen first and you can override it in part: a tap on a mana source on the table adds it or takes it out (in a stack of identical sources the topmost one not paying yet, or the one added last when all pay, TBL-130; in a stack of identical sources Autotap too takes the topmost ones). A source you pick stays picked and Autotap fills in the rest, one you take out isn't used again in this cast, and picking everything overrides it completely. (r74)
- **STK-159** A source a tap adds pays its sensible way: the first after which the rest of the cost can still be paid (Automatum's own check of the whole payment), and when the spell cares about the colours spent on it (converge, sunburst, "if {R} was spent", adamant) the way that serves that is tried first (a colour not spent yet; the colour it reads). It keeps its way while the rest can be paid with it, and changes colour only when the rest needs it. (r74)
- **STK-160** Each source paying carries a small amber chip at its top right corner with the mana it pays ("{U}", "Convoke"; "{C}" for colourless), and a closed pile of several paying sources one chip for the pile with all the mana it produces ("{R}{R}{R}"; "Convoke ×2"), taking no taps. (r74)
- **STK-161** When a paying source can pay in more than one way (a source of several colours, several mana abilities, a mana ability and convoke) a tap on its chip opens its ways beside it, the way it pays now marked, and a tap on one pays that way from then on, kept exactly, the rest worked out around it; a source with one way (improvise, delve, a basic land) has a chip that takes no taps. (r74)
- **STK-162** A source whose cost uses other cards on the battlefield (CR 605.3a: "Tap two untapped creatures you control: Add one mana", the creature an altar sacrifices; cards elsewhere: STK-214) is never offered as a choice naming cards, whether or not a payment was found: it is tapped on the table like any other source, its cost using the cards Automatum lists, and the payment's red line says what is still owed and that the mana is produced on the board. (r78)
- **STK-163** Cast (the main button, STK-052) is greyed while the chosen sources don't pay, with what is still owed in red ("Still owed {R}: produce the mana on the board.", "Not enough to pay {2}{R}: produce more mana on the board."). A choice that changes the cost keeps your picks and works Autotap's out again, and a cast reaching its payment with the chosen sources short (an X raised past them) stays open there. (r74)
- **STK-164** Before the payment is open, the payment section and the table's outlines show the payment the cast comes to at its smallest cost, with X at its least until you change it in the panel and then with the X you set (each + or − or typed value works the payment out again). Nothing is tapped while you choose: mana abilities run when the cast is finally made and goes on the Stack, and mana left over goes to your pool. (r74)
- **STK-059** While paying (a cast, an activation, or a payment while something resolves: ward, "counter unless you pay", echo) you can use every mana ability and no other ability (CR 605.3a), with what it needs: a pain land's coloured ability (STK-165), one that deals more, a land removing a counter, a creature an altar sacrifices, a colour or an amount, a filter land's colours, a Signet's own {1} from another source (its chain counted by what it nets, whichever order you picked them), mana abilities of a card in your hand, and what triggered mana abilities add when a land is tapped for mana (STK-166). (r62)
- **STK-211** A mana ability whose cost uses cards (collecting evidence, exiling a card from your graveyard, discarding a card) is offered while paying only when you have the cards it needs; while another mana ability you could use first might bring what it lacks (a discard for mana filling your graveyard), it stays offered. (r78)
- **STK-214** Which cards such a cost uses is yours to choose: a tap on its source on the table opens the cast panel's choice of them (the rows of a cast's own collecting evidence, STK-191, with Cancel) and its OK pays with them. Autotap's own pick takes the first ones that are enough; the payment line names the cards a source uses, with Change to choose again. (r78)
- **STK-165** A pain land's damage comes as its coloured ability runs and counts even when lethal: the cast then finishes and you lose when priority would next come; Undo can take it back (TRN-065). Paid life you don't have doesn't count. (r62)
- **STK-166** What triggered mana abilities add when a land is tapped for mana comes at once, never on the Stack; a colour they let you choose is said with the payment, or asked when you tap the land on its own. (r62)
- **STK-167** Mana choices interact with the cast's other costs (CR 601.2g before 601.2h): a land tapped for mana can then be sacrificed as the cost, a creature the cost taps can't also tap for mana, a pain land's damage comes before a paid-life cost (at 2 life it can't be combined with "pay 2 life", at 3 it can), a creature sacrificed for mana can't also be sacrificed for the cost. (r62)
- **STK-049** A mana cost paid while something resolves ("unless its controller pays {1}", "you may pay {1}", a cost to attack or block) is paid on the table as a cast's payment is (STK-024): a slim bar above your hand in the target bar's look (STK-047) titled "Pay {1}" over the source's name and the sentence it asks about (an ability's whole text, STK-226), "Paid" or "Not paid yet" beside the title, pool mana as small cards (STK-228) and life for a Phyrexian symbol as a chip in the bar, "Don't pay" in the bar where the effect lets you decline, and the source at its right. (r62, owner 2026-11-22)
- **STK-168** For a payment while something resolves, the sources Automatum may use are tapped on the table, those paying outlined in amber in a pile of their own with their corner chips (a tap adds a source its sensible way or takes it out, the chip changes its way, STK-024), Autotap's choice already chosen when it is on, none when off, and the tapped sources pay as a set, whatever order they were tapped in. Pay is the main button (TRN-063), greyed until they pay. (r62)

## How a cast shows and when it is dropped

- **STK-025** An open cast is the caster's alone: nothing reaches the other player until Cast; Back or Undo drops it without a trace (there is no Cancel) (Undo's point is the cast's start: you are just before you began it, where Undo would take you after it was made, HIS-084). (r62)
- **STK-169** While a cast is open, other game actions are refused: what they were made on shakes, saying "Finish the cast first, or drop it with Undo." (TBL-212), and the main button holds the cast's own Cast or Done (TRN-063), or is greyed when the cast has nothing for it ("Finish the cast first, or drop it with Undo."). While you can't act (timeline, offline) Cast sends nothing: "You can't cast right now (reviewing the timeline, or offline). The cast stays open." (r62)
- **STK-042** A cast shows on your device at once: its Stack entry appears and its sources tap (animated) while Automatum works out what follows (the passes, the spell resolving), with no spinner; then that outcome replaces it and goes to the server. Other game actions wait meanwhile (STK-025). If the cast can't go out after all or the server refuses it, the table goes back as with any refused action. (r42)
- **STK-055** When the board changes under an open cast because the other player acted, the cast is worked out again on the new board, keeping the choices that still fit (your mana tapping too, while its sources are there), or dropped, its card shaking. (r43)
- **STK-210** An undo or rewind that takes back a board you were shown (the other player's undo, one of theirs you accepted, a branch) drops an open cast at once, its card shaking, even where it could be cast again; an open choice of the game's (STK-030) then begins anew, nothing picked. (r43)
