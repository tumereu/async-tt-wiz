# Drafting

Cubes, pods of 2–8 players drafting asynchronously, the pick and grid screens, Build, Stats, and pod games.

### Cubes

- **DRF-001** Manage decks has two tabs, Decks | Cubes; the Cubes tab lists "CUBES (N)" with a New cube button at the top right. (r21)
- **DRF-002** A cube is a saved, named, counted card list with Main and Maybe sections (no Side), synced across your devices; drafting never uses it up and many pods can share one cube. Maybe holds cards being considered: it is never drafted, and only Prepare cube (DRF-141) uses it. (r35)
- **DRF-003** The cube editor is the deck editor (LOB-059, LOB-105, LOB-142, LOB-106) with the cube name in place of name and cover; Cube Cobra is its link source and search adds to Main or Maybe. (r35)
- **DRF-004** A pasted cube list is checked by itself shortly after you stop typing; there is no separate "Import list" button. (r20)
- **DRF-005** Cube lists accept every Cube Cobra export (plaintext, MTGO, Forge, XMage, CSV) and bare card names (one copy per line); maybeboard rows and a pasted list's sideboard lines go to Maybe. (r35)
- **DRF-006** A name shared by different cards does not block a cube: one is picked and listed with the other substitutions before saving; unknown cards block saving and name the bad lines. (r19)
- **DRF-007** A cube holds at most 3000 copies, counting Main and Maybe; the Cards tab line ("N main · M maybe", LOB-107) shows the copies before saving. (r35)
- **DRF-008** The top bar's "Import from link" dialog takes a Cube Cobra link or cube ID found anywhere in pasted text (https/www optional, query ignored); its paste icon pastes and imports at once. (r20)
- **DRF-009** A Cube Cobra import replaces the list: the mainboard goes to Main and the maybeboard to Maybe, and the cube's basics are ignored; an unnamed cube gets the Cube Cobra ID as its name; failures say why. (r35)
- **DRF-010** A saved cube opens in the saved list view (LOB-056: the picture view with a columns control), showing Main and Maybe; its columns start at 5 in portrait and 8 in landscape, remembered apart from decks. Above the cards: Edit at the top, then the name with "N total copies" and "Draft this cube", which opens New pod with that cube chosen; Delete cube is in the cube editor (LOB-057). (r35)
- **DRF-161** Cubes and draft sets get the deck warning icon (LOB-115) when Automatum can't play any card in them yet (the same words): in the Cubes tab, the saved cube view, the cube editor, the Source dialog's tabs, and by the source in New pod and pod setup (a cube there only when chosen from your cubes). A cube's result is worked out by the server like a deck's (LOB-124); a set's is the card catalog's mark (SYS-082); both known at once. (r57)
- **DRF-162** A source with DRF-161's warning can't start a pod or draft: New pod's Create and pod setup's Start draft and auto-start are greyed with "Automatum can't play every card of this source yet. Hold the warning sign to see which." The server refuses creating a pod from, or switching it to, such a cube or set, keeps the result with the pod, and refuses Start draft, auto-start and challenges from it. A cube's result is the server's, from its cards' marks when the cube is chosen (LOB-124); a set's is the card catalog's mark (SYS-082); the same on the phone and the server, and a set the catalog doesn't list for drafting is refused too.

### Pods list and navigation

- **DRF-011** The Pods screen (main menu: LOB-015) has a New pod button at the top right. (r21)
- **DRF-012** The Pods screen shows "MY PODS (N)" (N counts your pods) above one list in the shared pod order (DRF-013): pod rows with the compact stack of the other members' avatars (up to two plus "+N"; only yourself when alone), the name and status (a small dot after it while the pod's chat holds messages of other members you haven't read, DRF-170), and pod invitations with Accept and Decline. (r62)
- **DRF-013** Pod statuses read "Your pick · 2 packs" (just "Your pick" in Rotisserie, Winston and Winchester), "Build your deck", "Setup", "Drafting" or "Deck submitted"; "Your pick" rows are highlighted. One pod order everywhere (Pods screen, menu panel, side menu), shaped like the game order (LOB-022): pods waiting for your pick or build (longest waiting first), then pod invitations to you (newest first), then the rest (newest first). (r35)

### Pod setup

- **DRF-016** New pod and pod setup use the same complete settings form; creating needs a name, a source and valid settings, with a full-width Create fixed at the bottom. (r17)
- **DRF-017** The pod name field has a shuffle button that suggests another random fantasy name. (r16)
- **DRF-018** For the creator the source is a button showing its name (or "No source") with a switch icon, opening a Source dialog with searchable Cubes | Sets tabs and a Manage cubes button; beside it is the Mix sets toggle (DRF-177). Members see the name alone. (r17)
- **DRF-019** Choosing a source closes the dialog and shows it at once; getting it ready finishes in the background, and a failure restores the previous source. (r17)
- **DRF-020** A full-width mode button shows the pod's mode with a switch icon; tapping it opens a popup list of Booster, Grid, Rochester, Rotisserie, Winston, Winchester and Sealed, each with its player range. Choosing one loads that mode's default settings, keeping the sideboard limit and Librarians (Grid always has no Librarians). Glimpse is a built-in preset of Booster, and a pod's mode always reads "Booster", also for pods created as Glimpse. (r35)
- **DRF-021** Defaults: Booster 3 packs × 15 cards, keep 1, burn 0; Glimpse 9 packs × 15, keep 1, burn 2; both discard at 0, 1 pack open at once; Grid 18 grids, 1 open at once; Rochester 3 packs per player × 15 cards, packs open at once one per player; Rotisserie 45 cards per player; Winston 3 packs per player, 3 piles, 1 table; Winchester 3 packs per player, 4 piles, 1 table; Sealed 6 boosters per player from a set, 45 cards per player from a cube. Winston and Winchester packs from a cube hold 15 cards. (r60)
- **DRF-022** Settings are square tiles (small label, big value); tapping one opens a dialog that saves only valid in-range values, with a short explanation of the setting and its limits. Rochester's "Open packs" tile reads "1 each" (one per player) until a number is set; its dialog has a square "1 each" toggle like the sideboard's ∞. Winston and Winchester have a "Tables" tile after Piles. (r60)
- **DRF-023** Limits: packs 1–30, pack size 1–30, keep at least 1, keep + burn at most the pack size, open packs up to the pack count; grids 1–60; Rochester, Winston and Winchester packs per player 1–15 and pack size 5–30; Rochester packs open at once 1 up to all (any count above the number of packs opens them all); Rotisserie and cube Sealed cards per player 5–225; Winston piles 2–6, Winchester piles 2–8; Winston and Winchester tables 1–4 (more than the players gives one table per player); set Sealed boosters per player 1–30. (r60)
- **DRF-024** The pack size tile (Booster, Rochester, Winston and Winchester) shows only for cubes and for set packs without a booster recipe; set recipes fix their own size. (r35)
- **DRF-025** Sideboard limit is a tile, default unlimited (∞), with a square ∞ toggle inside its dialog; 0 is allowed. It counts Side only, never Maybe. (r60)
- **DRF-026** "Color balancing" is a square On/Off toggle tile for cube Booster/Glimpse, default on: each pack gets a card of every colour when the cube allows. (r19)
- **DRF-027** "Fresh boosters" toggle tile (default on) is always shown and greyed out unless the pod drafts grids from a set. (r29)
- **DRF-028** Grid has a "Two picks each" option, only for 2 players. (r16)
- **DRF-029** Only the creator edits name, source and settings; members see them read-only. Edits apply at once without disabling anything and save in the background. A name that can't be saved (the emptied field) waits in the field until a name is typed; leaving the pod then keeps the last saved name and saves the other edits. (r17)
- **DRF-030** Setup lists members and pending invitations with "Members (N/L)", L the engine's player limit (DRF-150: 8, or 4 for Grid, Winston and Winchester); the creator invites through the shared player picker, up to 8 members and invitations together. (r35)
- **DRF-141** Before drafting starts, pod setup offers "Prepare cube": the same picture-and-swipe screen as deck preparation (LOB-047, LOB-048), moving cards between the cube's Main and Maybe (a swipe either way, SYS-083) for this pod only; the saved cube does not change (DRF-002). Main is what gets drafted. Whoever can edit the pod's settings can prepare it. (r62)
- **DRF-142** Prepare cube is the deck preparation screen (LOB-047–LOB-051) with the cube's Main and Maybe as its sections, the cube's column defaults (DRF-010) and no basic land steppers, titled "Prepare cube · <cube name>" with Done at the top right. Pod setup shows "Prepare cube" with "N main · M maybe" under the settings. A pod starts from its cube's own Main and Maybe; choosing a source again starts over. (r35)

### Presets

- **DRF-031** A Presets button on New pod and on the creator's pod setup opens a dialog listing the built-in preset Glimpse first, then your saved presets, with search; there are no preset chips. Booster is not a preset: it is the mode button's Booster (DRF-020). (r60)
- **DRF-032** A preset stores the draft settings and source under a name, a set source with its pack grid (DRF-177); rows read like "Booster · My cube · 3×15" ("Rotisserie · My cube · 45 cards each", "Sealed · Dominaria United · 6 boosters", a grid of several sets "Booster · RTR, GTC, DGM · 3 packs": its sets' codes in the order its packs first name them), and a deleted cube shows "Cube missing". Choosing a preset with a grid brings the grid back, shown as mixed packs; a preset of one set shows one set (DRF-177). The built-in Glimpse holds settings only and reads "Glimpse · Built-in · 9×15"; choosing it keeps the pod's current source. (r60)
- **DRF-033** "Save current settings as preset…" asks a name (up to 60 characters); an existing name asks "Replace preset"; rows can be renamed and deleted (confirmed). The built-in Glimpse row can't be renamed, replaced or deleted. (r60)
- **DRF-034** Presets are saved on the server, so they appear on all your devices; up to 200 presets. (r31)

### Starting, leaving and cancelling

- **DRF-035** The creator's "Start draft" needs at least 2 accepted members and no more than the engine allows (DRF-150), settings that fit them and a source with enough cards; there is no Ready step. (r35)
- **DRF-036** While invitations are pending the button is "Start when everyone accepts"; armed, it reads "Starts when N more accept · Cancel" and members see "Starts automatically when everyone accepts". (r21)
- **DRF-037** Auto-start begins the draft when the last invitee accepts; a decline or a failed start turns it off with a setup line like "Bob declined; auto-start is off" and a notification. (r21)
- **DRF-038** The pod ⋮ menu offers "Start now" while invitations are pending (confirmed; pending invitations are cancelled), Cancel draft while drafting (creator), and Destroy (creator) or Leave. (r21)
- **DRF-039** Leaving during the draft warns "This cancels the draft for all N players and discards all picks." and returns the pod to setup; the creator's Cancel draft does the same. (r16)
- **DRF-040** Cancelling keeps source, settings and remaining members; starting again deals a fresh shuffle from the same source. One completed draft per pod. (r16)
- **DRF-041** Leaving after the draft withdraws your unaccepted challenges; when only one member is left the pod is destroyed. Destroy is confirmed and removes the pod for everyone. (r16)
- **DRF-042** Games already started from a pod survive any leave, cancel or destroy and stay in the normal Games list. (r16)
- **DRF-150** Each engine has a player limit: Booster, Rochester, Rotisserie and Sealed 2–8; Grid, Winston and Winchester 2–4. Start is refused when more members have joined than the engine allows, with the reason under it ("Grid takes at most 4 players; 5 have joined."). (r35)
- **DRF-163** Auto-start can't be armed while members plus pending invitations exceed the engine's limit (DRF-150): "Grid takes at most 4 players; 5 are joined or invited.". An armed auto-start survives settings changes, but a change or invitation that puts the pod over the limit switches it off and tells the creator why ("Auto-start off — <pod>"; setup line "Too many players for Winston (at most 4); auto-start is off"). (r35)

### Draft rules (Booster and Glimpse)

- **DRF-043** Seating is random (the server keeps it; the draft history shows it, DRF-165). Each round opens "open packs" packs per player; a round's packs all pass one way and the direction flips each round. (r16)
- **DRF-044** A pick takes "keep" cards into your pool and removes "burn" cards from the draft; burned cards go to nobody. A confirmed pick cannot be undone. (r16)
- **DRF-045** A pack with keep cards or fewer left is given to its receiver automatically; with keep + burn + discard (DRF-046) or fewer you choose only the keeps and the rest go to nobody: up to burn of them burn and the others are thrown away. (r42)
- **DRF-046** After a pick leaves "discard" cards or fewer in the pack, the rest are thrown away; 0 means never. (r16)
- **DRF-047** You open your next round's packs as soon as no pack of your opened rounds can come back to you for a pick. (r20)
- **DRF-048** You never see a next-round pack while a pack of your current round can still reach you; such packs wait hidden and send no "your pick". (r21)
- **DRF-049** Your queue is ordered by round, then fresher packs (fewer picks taken) first, then arrival; an arrival never replaces the pack you are looking at. (r22)
- **DRF-050** Cube cards are drawn without replacement for the whole draft from the pod's prepared Main (DRF-141); a Main too small for the settings blocks Start, and using every card exactly is fine. (r35)

### Cogwork Librarian

- **DRF-051** Booster and Glimpse have a "Librarians" tile, 0–10, default 0: each player starts with that many Cogwork Librarians in Side, not in packs. (r18)
- **DRF-052** The keep tray ends with a Librarian chip showing how many are left; tapping it adds a dashed, badged keep slot and puts that Librarian into the pack as a card. (r29)
- **DRF-053** A spent Librarian shows a book badge; you may keep it back, burn it or leave it to pass on, also on a pack's last pick; Refund in its menu (or tapping its slot) takes it back. (r29)
- **DRF-054** Spending a Librarian never changes the tray layout; with Librarians the keep tray is always at least 2 cards wide and Confirm reads "Confirm · 1 Librarian". (r19)
- **DRF-055** An unspent or later-drafted Librarian is an ordinary card for deckbuilding and can be spent again; automatic awards never spend one. (r21)

### Pick screen

- **DRF-056** Pack cards scroll in a grid, cards per row 1–15 (default 3 portrait, 5 landscape, saved per device and orientation); keep/burn trays and a full-width Confirm stay fixed below. (r17)
- **DRF-057** The first line above the cards reads "Round 2/6 · Pick 2 · from Matti", or "· your pack" before its first pick, and scrolls away with them. (r22)
- **DRF-058** Swipe right = keep, left = burn: the card stays put while a 70% see-through copy follows the finger sideways, tilting up to 8° and tinting green/red. (r17)
- **DRF-059** Only releasing at 30% of the card width or more marks the card; the copy then fades in place in 150 ms (no snap-back) and the card flashes and gets its border and badge in 200 ms. (r17)
- **DRF-060** Swiping a marked card the same way unmarks it, the other way switches it; marking more than allowed drops the earliest mark of that kind; with burn 0 left swipes do nothing. (r16)
- **DRF-061** Marked cards keep their place with a green/red border hugging the art on all sides and a ✓/🔥 badge; swiped cards draw above their neighbours. (r18)
- **DRF-062** Tapping a card opens a small menu: Keep, Burn, Unmark (as applicable), Refund Librarian; there is no Inspect (holding inspects, DRF-063) and no double-tap shortcut. (r52)
- **DRF-063** Holding a card still for 150 ms inspects it (TBL-104: every face, only card images) until release; moving within 300 ms of it appearing closes it and continues the swipe or scroll; after that only lifting the finger closes it, however the finger moves (a vertical move scrolls a tall Inspect). (r62)
- **DRF-064** Only one card menu or preview is open at a time across Pick, Grid and Build; the latest tap or hold wins, also with several fingers. (r19)
- **DRF-065** The keep and burn trays sit on one row when they fit, sharing spare width in proportion, with an icon + "x/y" chip in the corner; they pan sideways when full; tapping a tray card unmarks it. (r19)
- **DRF-066** The burn tray hides when no burns are needed; automatic burns near a pack's end appear only after all keeps are marked. (r17)
- **DRF-067** Confirm is enabled only when the counts are exactly right; after it the next queued pack shows at once while the pick sends in the background. (r16)
- **DRF-068** A refused pick restores that pack and its marks with a short message; an unconfirmed one offers Retry. (r16)
- **DRF-069** Your keep, burn and Librarian marks are kept when you switch tabs or close the app (saved on the server about half a second after the last change). (r22)
- **DRF-070** Card pictures for every card a draft screen shows (the shown pack and the next queued pack; the open Rochester packs, the Rotisserie pool, seen Winston piles, the Winchester piles and the Picks view) start downloading at once; missing pictures keep a named placeholder and retry forever. (r35)
- **DRF-071** With no pack to pick, a faded centred text shows, each part on its own line: "Waiting for <names>" then "N packs will come back to you", or "All your picks are done" then "<names> is finishing" ("Other players are finishing" when unknown); then "Round x of y" (Grid: "Waiting for <names> · Round x of y"). In Rochester, Rotisserie, Winston and Winchester the board stays on screen and its header line names whose pick it is ("Waiting for <name>"). (r35)
- **DRF-072** Under the waiting text a borderless "Main menu" link leaves the pod; there is no Build button there. (r20)

### Pod header and tabs

- **DRF-074** While drafting each avatar has a badge: a green ✓ when that member can't pick, otherwise how many picks they can make in a row (next-round packs they would open included), "9+" above 9. (r32)
- **DRF-075** A cogwheel at the header's end opens a floating panel with every view setting, applied live: "Draft · cards per row" (not in Grid), "History · cards per row" (Booster and Glimpse, DRF-165), "Build · columns", "Build · cards side by side". (r60)
- **DRF-076** Tabs are Draft · Build · Stats while drafting and Build · Stats · Games afterwards, with the pod ⋮ menu as a narrow last tab; Back leaves the pod. (r18)
- **DRF-077** A pod opens on Draft while drafting, on Build until your deck is submitted, then on Games. (r17)
- **DRF-078** Pod screens refresh quietly in the background (about every 10 s while open), with no loaders over shown data. (r17)

### Grid draft

- **DRF-079** Grid is Draftmancer's: 2–4 players, 3×3 grids; on your turn take every card of one row or column; with 3–4 players only the first pick's line is refilled. The turn order is Draftmancer's: 2 players alternate who starts; 3 players go through all six orders, one per grid; 4 players through the four rotations and then the four reversed ones. (r35)
- **DRF-080** All grids are dealt at the start with their own refill cards; you open your next grids as soon as none of your open grids can come back to you; grids of unopened rounds stay hidden. (r30)
- **DRF-081** Every grid you have seen shows as dealt with cards in place: your current pick first, then other open grids, then finished grids (latest first). (r30)
- **DRF-082** Each grid has a header: "Your pick · Grid 4/18", "Your pick next · Grid 6/18", "Waiting for <name> · Grid 5/18" or "Finished · Grid 3/18". (r30)
- **DRF-083** Only the grid you pick from has arrows beside each row and above each column; tapping one tints that line, slightly dims the other cards, and Confirm takes it. (r31)
- **DRF-084** Tapping a grid card does nothing; holding it inspects it with the same 150 ms release-to-close hold as the pick screen. (r52)
- **DRF-085** Each pick shows as a coloured band along its row or column (yours in your table colour, others in opponent colour, then blue and amber) plus the picker's small avatar in the card's top left corner, over the name. (r34)
- **DRF-086** Discarded cards of a finished grid are dimmed and refill cards are marked "New"; a refilled grid shows its "Before refill" and "After refill" boards framed together. (r34)
- **DRF-087** Grid boards and everyone's picks of grids you have seen are public to the pod. (r16)

### Rochester, Rotisserie, Winston, Winchester and Sealed

- **DRF-151** Sealed has no draft phase: at the start every player gets a private pool (set: their own boosters opened; cube: random cards without replacement) and the pod goes straight to Build with "Draft complete: build your deck". (r35)
- **DRF-152** Rotisserie: the whole pool lies face up (cube: players × cards per player, drawn at random; set: boosters opened until there are enough, all their cards); players take one card per turn in snake order (seat order, then back) until each has their cards; turns are sequential. (r35)
- **DRF-153** The Rotisserie pick screen is one scrolling panel of the whole pool, sorted like Build columns (W U B R G, multicolour, colourless, then name), under a line like "Your pick · Pick 4 of 90" ("Waiting for <name> · …" on another's turn) and a "Search cards" field using the catalog search syntax; taken cards stay greyed with the picker's small avatar (as in Grid). On your turn tap a card to frame it, then "Pick <card name>" takes it; on another's turn tap a free card you haven't queued to frame it, then "Queue <card name>" adds it to your pick queue (DRF-164); other taps do nothing; holding inspects. (r60)
- **DRF-164** Rotisserie pick queue: your queue is private and kept on the server, so it works while you are away. A strip above the button lists it in order as chips ("1 · Lightning Bolt"), each with an ✕ that removes it, and queued cards in the pool carry their number in a small badge. When your turn comes the queue's first card is taken for you at once, with no "Your pick" notification and no wait, and the rest stays queued in order (a run of picks in a row takes one queued card each). If another player takes any queued card your whole queue clears and the strip reads "Queue cleared: <card> was taken" until you queue or pick again; your turn then waits for you as usual. (r60)
- **DRF-154** Rochester: packs are opened face up and everyone sees every open pack; each pack is picked one card per turn in snake order starting from its opener, until it is empty. Several packs are open at once (default one per player): pack openers rotate around the table, you pick in every open pack where it is your turn, and the next round's packs open when the current ones are all empty; each pack's first lap goes in seat order. (r35)
- **DRF-155** The Rochester pick screen shows "Round x of y" and then every open pack in one scrolling view, your packs to pick from first, then the others by number, each under a divider and its header ("Your pick · Pack 4", "Waiting for <name> · Pack 5", "Finished · Pack 3"). Taken cards stay in place, greyed, with the picker's small avatar (as in Grid); tap a card of a pack where it is your pick to frame it, then "Pick <card name>"; other taps do nothing and holding inspects. (r35)
- **DRF-156** Winston: the packs' cards form a face-down stack; the piles (default 3) start with one card each. On your turn you look at pile 1: take it (it gets one new card from the stack) or add a stack card to it and look at the next pile; passing the last pile takes the stack's top card. Empty piles are skipped; with the stack empty you can't pass when no later pile has cards, and its last card goes to you, not onto the last pile. Turns go round the table one whole turn at a time. Piles you have looked at stay face up for you; cards added later stay face down until you look again. With k tables (the "Tables" setting, default 1, as Winchester's, DRF-158) the shuffled cards split evenly into k independent tables, each with its own stack and piles, played by these rules, and its own turn order starting one seat later per table; the draft ends when every table's stack and piles are empty. (r60)
- **DRF-157** The Winston pick screen lists the piles one under another ("Pile 2 · 3 cards", "· you look at it" or "· Bob looks at it" on the pile in play), cards you have seen face up and the rest as card backs, under a line like "Your pick · Stack: 31 cards"; on your turn "Pass" ("Pass · take top card" on the last pile) and "Take pile N" sit at the bottom. Tapping a card does nothing; holding inspects it. With several tables (DRF-156) a chip row on top ("Table 1 · Your pick", "Table 2 · Bob looks", "Table 3 · Finished") shows one table at a time, the line, piles and buttons being that table's; the screen opens on a table where it is your pick and, after your turn there ends, moves to one as soon as there is one (a pass stays; a tap on a chip shows that table instead). One table shows no chip row. (r60)
- **DRF-158** Winchester: the packs' cards are shuffled into a face-down stack; there are N face-up piles (default 4). Each turn every pile gets one card from the stack, then the player whose turn it is takes one whole pile; turns go round the table, and the draft ends when the stack and piles are empty. With k tables (the "Tables" setting, default 1, like Booster's packs open at once) the shuffled cards split evenly into k independent tables, each with its own stack and N piles, dealt and taken by these rules, and its own turn order starting one seat later per table (with 2 players and 2 tables the first seat starts at table 1, the second at table 2, so both can pick at once); the draft ends when every table's stack and piles are empty. (r60)
- **DRF-159** The Winchester pick screen lists the face-up piles one under another ("Pile 2 · 3 cards") under a line like "Your pick · Stack: 24 cards"; on your turn tap a card of a pile to frame that pile, then "Take pile N (k cards)"; other taps do nothing, and holding a card inspects it. With several tables (DRF-158) a chip row on top ("Table 1 · Your pick", "Table 2 · Bob picks", "Table 3 · Finished") shows one table's piles at a time, the line, pile headers and "Take pile N" being that table's; the screen opens on a table where it is your pick and, after you take a pile, moves to one as soon as there is one (a tap on a chip shows that table instead). One table shows no chip row. (r60)
- **DRF-160** Winston, Winchester and Grid have a "Picks" button at the top right of the Draft tab; it opens a dialog with a chip per opponent (avatar, name and card count) and that opponent's picks, in order, newest first. A card shows face up if you have seen it (always in Winchester and Grid; in Winston only cards you had seen in a pile), otherwise as a card back; holding a card inspects it, over the dialog. Booster and Glimpse have no such button; their History is in the pod's header (DRF-165). Rochester and Rotisserie show picks on the board itself. (r60)
- **DRF-165** Booster and Glimpse: the History icon in the pod's header beside the cogwheel, on every tab (the Draft tab included; the Draft tab has no History button of its own), during the draft, while building and after it, opens a full-screen "Draft history" with every pick of yours round by round, oldest first within a round (a round's last card given automatically shows in its own round even when it came after you opened the next), each round under a header naming whom its packs passed to ("Round 1 · passed to Bob"; with three or more players also the way round, "Round 2 · passed to Carol · counter-clockwise": round 1 passes to the next seat, clockwise, and the direction flips each round, DRF-043) with a thick divider before every round but the first, and each pick under a header "Pack 1 · Pick 3" ("Pack 2 · Pick 14 · given automatically" for an automatic award): the pack as it came to you (Librarians you spent on it included), sorted like Build columns, your keeps with a green border and ✓, your burns red with 🔥. When a pack came round to you before, the cards gone from it since you passed it follow under "Taken since you last saw it", greyed. "Returning packs only", a chip in the header, shows only the packs that came back to you a second or later time in their round (a round without one isn't shown; "No packs have come back to you yet" when there is none), remembered per pod on the device. At the top, under the header, a ring of the pod's avatars shows the seating (you at the bottom, then the seats clockwise in seat order) with arrowheads on the ring for the direction of the round at the top of the list, and beside it that round's header line. The server keeps the history and the seating and tells only what your own packs showed. It shows "Loading…" until it comes and "Couldn't load the draft history" with Retry on a failure; holding a card inspects it, over the dialog. Its cards per row is its own setting (DRF-075): 1 up to the pack size (1–15 for sets with a booster recipe), default 8 portrait and 15 landscape, capped at the pack size, saved per device and orientation. (r70)

### Set boosters

- **DRF-088** The Sets tab lists the card records' draftable sets only (expansion, core, masters, draft innovation), already released, newest first, each with its set symbol before its name (DRF-176), searchable by name, code or release date. A set Automatum can't play every card of (the card catalog's marks, SYS-082) is greyed and can't be chosen, DRF-161's warning sign beside it. (r17)
- **DRF-089** Choosing a set chooses it at once: nothing is downloaded for it, and the pod gets it by its code (DRF-169). Every set row looks the same apart from DRF-161's warning sign (no Redownload button). (r60)
- **DRF-167** Sets with meld cards can be drafted, and decks can hold them: each front is a card of its own with the meld result on its back. The result alone is never a card in a pack; a deck line naming only the result gets its first front.
- **DRF-090** Each set pack follows its own set's (DRF-179) Play Booster, else Draft Booster, else default recipe; without one they are approximate (10 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 rare or mythic, 1 any), each card of the set counted once (a double-faced or other multi-face card too, and a card with several printings in the set). A sheet of cards from other sets holding a card Automatum can't play yet (special guests, The List) is left out, its slots filled from the pack's main sheet (the one it draws most cards from). Set packs never run out. Each card is the printing its pack drew: the one its recipe's sheet names (that set's variants included, so the same card may differ in art; a sheet of other sets' cards, kept, gives those printings), and in an approximate pack one of that set's printings of the card drawn, at random; a set's card never comes as another set's printing. The card stays that printing in the pool, Build and the saved draft deck (LOB-040); a cube pod's cards are the cube's printings. (r16)
- **DRF-091** With Fresh boosters on, every grid from a set gets its own booster and nine random cards from it. (r29)
- **DRF-169** A pod's sets are named by their codes alone: one set for the whole pod, or the pack grid of set codes read row by row (DRF-177); the server builds each pack (DRF-090) from its set in its card catalog (SYS-081), with no limit on their cards or sheets.
- **DRF-176** Every set shows its set symbol, drawn on the phone without a connection by the Keyrune icon font that comes with the app, from the card records' set data (MTGJSON's Keyrune code): in the Sets tab before its name (DRF-088) and in every cell of the pack grid over its code (DRF-177). A set the font has no symbol for shows its code as text instead.
- **DRF-177** A pod from a set has one set for every pack by default. Beside the source button (DRF-018) the creator has the "Mix sets" toggle, an icon (holding it says what a tap does), switching the form between one set and mixed packs. One set: the chosen set fills every pack, also packs added later. Mixed packs show the pod's pack grid: one row per round and one cell per pack of that round, sized by the pod's settings (Booster: its rounds of open packs; Grid: its rounds of open grids, a cell per grid; Rochester: a round per pack each player opens; Sealed, Winston and Winchester: one row, "All packs"), with column labels "Pack 1", "Pack 2"… and row labels "Round 1"…; each cell is a set chip. The order in a row is the order that round's packs are opened. Switching only changes what the form shows, never a pack: back in one set after mixing, the source reads "Mixed sets" until a set is chosen in the Source dialog, which then fills every pack. A grid of more than one set opens as mixed packs, members always see it (read-only), and the source then reads "Mixed sets"; the toggle is the creator's own view, kept while the pod's setup is open. A cube and Rotisserie (which takes one set) have no mixed packs, and no set chosen yet has none to mix: the toggle stays, greyed, and holding it says why ("Only sets can be mixed, not a cube", "Rotisserie takes one set", "Choose a set first").
- **DRF-178** Tapping a cell of the pack grid opens the set list titled for it ("Set for Round 2 · Pack 1") to choose that pack's set; tapping a row label ("Round 2") chooses the set of every pack of that round, a column label ("Pack 1") that pack in every round. As in the Sets tab, only a set Automatum can play every card of can be chosen. Choosing a set in the Source dialog makes it the set of every pack again.
- **DRF-179** The server builds every pack from its cell's set: each player's packs are the grid read row by row (Booster, Rochester, Winston and Winchester), each Sealed booster and each grid (with or without fresh boosters) its own cell's. It refuses the whole grid when any of its sets can't be drafted (DRF-162). New settings resize the grid: more packs take the last pack's set, fewer drop the last packs.

(r61)
- **DRF-095** Columns sit side by side filling the width: Lands, then mana value 0, 1, 2 … with the last column "and above"; 2–10 columns, default 7, saved per device and orientation. (r16)
- **DRF-096** "Build · cards side by side" puts 1–3 cards next to each other in every column, default 1 in both orientations. (r22)
- **DRF-097** Column labels show the column's card count on a line under the label; columns are faintly tinted, cards whole (never overlapped), 1 dp apart, with wide side margins. (r23)

- **DRF-099** Swipes (SYS-083): Main right→Maybe, left→Side; Side right→Main, left→Maybe; Maybe right→Side, left→Main. (r62)
- **DRF-100** While swiping the card stays put and a 70% see-through tilted copy tinted in the destination colour follows the finger; under 20 dp it springs back. (r20)
- **DRF-101** A moved card fades out where it was and in at its new place while others slide (~250 ms); a brief chip like "→ Side" confirms the move. (r20)
- **DRF-102** Tapping a card opens a menu with its two destinations; after submitting they are disabled. Holding inspects the card like the pick screen. (r52)
- **DRF-103** A W/U/B/R/G basics row under Main (and one under Side) opens one dialog with five −/+ steppers and Done, with "Basics N · Lands M" left of Done updating live (the section it was opened for, M counting every land there, drafted ones too); basics are unlimited and not drafted. (r23)
- **DRF-104** New drafts with an unlimited sideboard start with 17 of each basic in Side; Side basics count toward a finite sideboard limit. (r23)
- **DRF-166** A cover thumbnail sits beside the deck's name in Build; a tap lists your Main and Side cards alphabetically, basics not among them, to choose it from, also after submitting. It is private as the name is (DRF-125) and goes to your draft deck (LOB-040), to your games of the pod still in their lobby and to the pod games prepared after it; it can also be chosen from Prepare deck in a pod game's lobby (LOB-047); without a choice, or once the chosen card has gone to Maybe, the first Main card shows. (r62)
- **DRF-105** The deck name row has a pen button; the name (default "<pod name> deck") is private to you and can be changed even after submitting, here or from Prepare deck in a pod game's lobby (LOB-047); a new name reaches your draft deck and your games of the pod still in their lobby. (r70)
- **DRF-106** A fixed bottom bar across the full screen width (not inset by the columns' side margins) holds an Undo button at its left edge (always there, dimmed when empty) and "Submit deck (N)"; Undo takes back card moves and basic changes one at a time, during and after the draft. (r36)

### Stats

- **DRF-111** Stats covers Main including basics: a mana curve 0–6 and 7+ of nonland cards with totals, and "Creature / Noncreature / Land" counts. A small Type | Color toggle above the chart (remembered on the device, Type at first) stacks the bars creature/noncreature (each bar's number reading "creatures · noncreatures", "2 · 3"), or W U B R G, gold (more than one colour) and colourless by each card's colour as in the donut (DRF-117), in the donut's colours and with a legend of its own, each bar's number its total; the chart's height and slots stay the same. (r62)
- **DRF-112** A type row counts Main cards per supertype and card type word of the front type line (e.g. Legendary, Creature, Basic, Land; subtypes like Golem have their own row, DRF-180), showing only words present, most common first. (r32)
- **DRF-113** Type row details: hidden when Main is empty, words as printed, each a grey pill with a bold count. (r32)
- **DRF-180** Under the type row a subtype row counts Main cards per subtype of the front type line (every subtype for now: creature types, Equipment, Aura, …; the basics' land types, Plains to Forest, included), most common first (ties by name), in the type row's look (DRF-113): the first six, then a "+N more" chip whose tap shows the rest, with "Fewer" after them to hide them again (all show when only one more would hide). (r70)
- **DRF-114** A table shows per colour W U B R G its counts and shares in two lines, "9 pips · 34%" and "8 sources · 30%" (half pips like "4½ pips"; sources being lands and other producers added up), and two bars, pips over sources: the colour's share of all coloured pips and its share of all sources (the sum over the colours, a land making two colours counting for both). The full width is 100% of all pips and of all sources, nothing scaled to the largest, so what is drawn sums to one whole and an imbalance shows by eye; a colour with a share of 0 keeps its row. The pip bar is thin and dark, the source bar thicker, its solid part lands and a lighter part other producers, with a small legend at the top; under the rows a total line "Sources: 17 lands, 3 others" (lands making a colour, basics included, and other producers), plus "Lands without a color: N". (r61)
- **DRF-115** Sources come from Automatum's reading of the cards (the same card definitions as the source check, DRF-161): the colours the front face's mana abilities can add (CR 605), counted for lands (with their basic land types, and basics added in Build) and for nonland permanents (mana creatures, mana rocks) apart; a card making several colours (one at a time, "any color", or a colour chosen as it enters) counts 1 for each colour it can make. Spells that add mana (rituals) and cards that only create Treasure or other mana tokens don't count, and a card without a definition makes no colour beyond its basic land types. (r60)
- **DRF-116** Under it, cards are grouped by their coloured mana symbols as chips flowing in rows ("3× {R}{R}"), with a "N× ∅" chip last for cards without coloured pips. (r26)
- **DRF-117** A Colors donut shows Main nonland cards by colour (W U B R G, multicolour, colourless) with percentages. (r18)
- **DRF-118** Stats has private deck notes (up to 2000 characters) and eight tally counters (W U B R G, colourless, gold, grey; 0–99), saved for this draft and visible only to you. (r23)

- **DRF-120** Submitting locks Main, Side, Maybe and basics; the bar then shows "✓ Submitted". Maybe comes along to pod games outside the game with Side (LOB-141). Members submit independently. (r60)
- **DRF-121** The Games tab lists members as "deck submitted" or "building" and, once yours is submitted, a crossed-swords Challenge button beside every other member; then the pod's Standings (each member's W–L–D in the pod's finished matches, most wins first, then fewest losses, then most draws) and its Matches, played or being played: "<name> vs <name>" with "In play" and the turn reached, or the winner ("<name> won", "Draw", "Unfinished") with date, turns and how it ended; no deck information, also for your own matches; then this pod's games. - **DRF-124** In a pod game's lobby each deck is fixed to its pod deck (no saved-deck choice); its basics move between Main and Side like other cards (LOB-052); Ready checks 40 Main cards and the sideboard limit. (r55)
- **DRF-125** A pod deck's name is shown only to its owner unless they switch on "Show deck name & art" for that game. (r31)

### Pod chat

A small dot on the button shows messages of other members you haven't read; seeing them in the chat reads them. - **DRF-171** Only the pod's members read and write its chat: not an invited player before accepting, and not a member who left (their messages stay, with their name).
