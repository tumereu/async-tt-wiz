# Drafting

Cubes, pods drafting asynchronously, pod setup and presets, the draft modes and their rules, the pick and grid
screens, Build and its review, Stats, submitting and pod games, pod chat, notifications and privacy.

## How it works

A cube is your own saved card list to draft from; a pod can also draft boosters of a card set. A pod is a group of
players who draft together, each picking whenever they have time: its creator chooses the source, the draft mode and
its settings, invites players and starts the draft. During the draft you see only your own packs (some modes lay their
cards out for everyone) and pick on the pick screen, while the server deals, passes and keeps track of everything.
Your picks land in Build, where you sort your cards into Main, Side and Maybe and add basic lands, with Stats to check
the curve and colours. Once your own draft is done (nothing more can reach you) you submit your deck, also while others
still draft, and the pod's members challenge each other to games with their pod decks. Each pod has its own chat, and notifications tell you when it is your pick.

## Cubes

- **DRF-002** A cube is a saved, named, counted card list with Main and Maybe sections (no Side), synced across your devices; drafting never uses it up and many pods can share one cube. Maybe holds cards being considered: it is never drafted, and only Prepare cube (DRF-141) uses it. (r35)
- **DRF-003** The cube editor is the deck editor (LOB-059, LOB-105, LOB-142, LOB-106) with the cube name in place of name and cover; Cube Cobra is its link source and search adds to Main or Maybe. (r35)

- **DRF-005** Cube lists accept every Cube Cobra export (plaintext, MTGO, Forge, XMage, CSV) and bare card names (one copy per line); maybeboard rows and a pasted list's sideboard lines go to Maybe. (r35)
- **DRF-006** A name shared by different cards does not block a cube: one is picked and listed with the other substitutions before saving; unknown cards block saving and name the bad lines. (r19)
- **DRF-007** A cube holds at most 3000 copies, counting Main and Maybe; the Cards tab line ("N main · M maybe", LOB-107) shows the copies before saving. (r35)
- **DRF-008** The top bar's "Import from link" dialog takes a Cube Cobra link or cube ID found anywhere in pasted text (https/www optional, query ignored); its paste icon pastes and imports at once. (r20)

- **DRF-010** A saved cube opens in the saved list view (LOB-056: the picture view with a columns control), showing Main and Maybe; its columns start at 5 in portrait and 8 in landscape, remembered apart from decks. (r35)

## Sets as a source

- **DRF-088** The Sets tab lists the card records' draftable sets only (expansion, core, masters, draft innovation), already released, newest first, each with its set symbol before its name (DRF-176), searchable by name, code or release date. A set Automatum can't play every card of (the card catalog's marks, SYS-082) is greyed and can't be chosen, DRF-161's warning sign beside it. (r17)
- **DRF-176** Every set shows its set symbol, drawn on the phone without a connection by the Keyrune icon font that comes with the app, from the card records' set data (MTGJSON's Keyrune code): in the Sets tab before its name (DRF-088) and in every cell of the pack grid over its code (DRF-177). A set the font has no symbol for shows its code as text instead.
- **DRF-089** Choosing a set chooses it at once: nothing is downloaded for it, and the pod gets it by its code (DRF-169). Every set row looks the same apart from DRF-161's warning sign (no Redownload button). (r60)
- **DRF-169** A pod's sets are named by their codes alone: one set for the whole pod, or the pack grid of set codes read row by row (DRF-177); the server builds each pack (DRF-090) from its set in its card catalog (SYS-081), with no limit on their cards or sheets.
- **DRF-090** Each set pack follows its own set's (DRF-179) Play Booster, else Draft Booster, else default recipe; without one they are approximate (10 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 rare or mythic, 1 any), each card of the set counted once (a double-faced or other multi-face card too, and a card with several printings in the set). Set packs never run out. (r16)
- **DRF-184** A sheet of cards from other sets holding a card Automatum can't play yet (special guests, The List) is left out of a set pack, its slots filled from the pack's main sheet (the one it draws most cards from). (r16)
- **DRF-185** Each card of a set pack is the printing its pack drew: the one its recipe's sheet names (that set's variants included, so the same card may differ in art; a sheet of other sets' cards, kept, gives those printings), and in an approximate pack one of that set's printings of the card drawn, at random; a set's card never comes as another set's printing. (r16)
- **DRF-186** A drafted card stays the printing its pack drew in the pool, Build and the saved draft deck (LOB-040); a cube pod's cards are the cube's printings. (r16)
- **DRF-167** Sets with meld cards can be drafted, and decks can hold them: each front is a card of its own with the meld result on its back. The result alone is never a card in a pack; a deck line naming only the result gets its first front.

## Sources Automatum can't play yet

- **DRF-161** Cubes and draft sets get the deck warning icon (LOB-115) when even one of their cards is one Automatum can't play yet (LOB-144; the same words), a cube's Maybe cards counting as its Main ones: in the Cubes tab, the saved cube view, the cube editor, the Source dialog's tabs, and by the source in New pod and pod setup (a cube there only when chosen from your cubes). (r57)
- **DRF-187** A cube's result (for the warning and for starting) is worked out by the server like a deck's, from its cards' marks when the cube is chosen (LOB-124); a set's is the card catalog's mark (SYS-082); both are known at once, and they are the same on the phone and the server. (r57)
- **DRF-162** A source with the warning (DRF-161), even for a card only in a cube's Maybe, can't start a pod or draft: New pod's Create and pod setup's Start draft and auto-start are greyed with "Automatum can't play every card of this source yet. Hold the warning sign to see which."
- **DRF-188** The server refuses creating a pod from, or switching it to, a cube or set Automatum can't play every card of, keeps the result with the pod, and refuses Start draft, auto-start and challenges from it; a set the catalog doesn't list for drafting is refused too.

Beside the source button (DRF-018) the creator has the "Mix sets" toggle, an icon (holding it says what a tap does), switching the form between one set and mixed packs. One set: the chosen set fills every pack, also packs added later.
- **DRF-195** Mixed packs show the pod's pack grid: one row per round and one cell per pack of that round, sized by the pod's settings (Booster: its rounds of open packs; Grid: its rounds of open grids, a cell per grid; Rochester: a round per pack each player opens; Arena: a row per pick, "Pick 1"…, DRF-286; Sealed, Winston and Winchester: one row, "All packs"), with column labels "Pack 1", "Pack 2"… and row labels "Round 1"…; each cell is a set chip. The order in a row is the order that round's packs are opened.
- **DRF-196** Switching between one set and mixed packs only changes what the form shows, never a pack: back in one set after mixing, the source reads "Mixed sets" until a set is chosen in the Source dialog, which then fills every pack.
- **DRF-197** A grid of more than one set opens as mixed packs, members always see it (read-only), and the source then reads "Mixed sets"; the Mix sets toggle is the creator's own view, kept while the pod's setup is open.
- **DRF-198** A cube and Rotisserie (which takes one set) have no mixed packs, and no set chosen yet has none to mix: the Mix sets toggle stays, greyed, and holding it says why ("Only sets can be mixed, not a cube", "Rotisserie takes one set", "Choose a set first").
- **DRF-178** Tapping a cell of the pack grid opens the set list titled for it ("Set for Round 2 · Pack 1") to choose that pack's set; tapping a row label ("Round 2") chooses the set of every pack of that round, a column label ("Pack 1") that pack in every round. As in the Sets tab, only a set Automatum can play every card of can be chosen. Choosing a set in the Source dialog makes it the set of every pack again.
- **DRF-179** The server builds every pack from its cell's set: each player's packs are the grid read row by row (Booster, Rochester, Winston and Winchester), each Sealed booster and each grid (with or without fresh boosters) its own cell's. It refuses the whole grid when any of its sets can't be drafted (DRF-162). New settings resize the grid: more packs take the last pack's set, fewer drop the last packs.

## Starting, leaving and cancelling

- **DRF-035** The creator's "Start draft" needs at least 2 seats, accepted members and bot seats together (the creator alone with bots is a solo draft, DRF-296; Sealed and Arena, without bots, need 2 members), and no more than the engine allows (DRF-150), settings that fit them and a source with enough cards; there is no Ready step. Auto-start (DRF-036) never starts a pod of one person. (r35; owner, 2026-11-23)
- **DRF-150** Each engine has a player limit: Booster, Custom booster, Rochester, Rotisserie, Sealed and Arena 2–8; Grid, Winston and Winchester 2–4. Bot seats count as players. Start is refused when more members have joined than the engine allows, with the reason under it ("Grid takes at most 4 players; 5 have joined.", with bots "Grid takes at most 4 players; 3 have joined, with 2 bots."). (r35)
- **DRF-036** While invitations are pending the button is "Start when everyone accepts"; armed, it reads "Starts when N more accept · Cancel" and members see "Starts automatically when everyone accepts". (r21)
- **DRF-037** Auto-start begins the draft when the last invitee accepts; a decline or a failed start turns it off with a setup line like "Bob declined; auto-start is off" and a notification. (r21)
- **DRF-163** Auto-start can't be armed while members plus pending invitations (plus bot seats) exceed the engine's limit (DRF-150): "Grid takes at most 4 players; 5 are joined or invited." (with bots "…; 3 are joined or invited, with 2 bots."). (r35)
- **DRF-203** An armed auto-start survives settings changes, but a change or invitation that puts the pod over the engine's limit switches it off and tells the creator why ("Auto-start off — <pod>"; setup line "Too many players for Winston (at most 4); auto-start is off"). (r35)
- **DRF-038** The pod ⋮ menu offers "Start now" while invitations are pending (confirmed; pending invitations are cancelled), Cancel draft while drafting (creator), and Destroy (creator) or Leave. (r21)
- **DRF-039** Leaving during the draft warns "This cancels the draft for all N players and discards all picks." and returns the pod to setup; the creator's Cancel draft does the same. Decks submitted early (DRF-119) are discarded with the picks, and the pod's challenges not yet accepted are withdrawn. Its games already accepted or in play carry on (DRF-042), orphaned from the pod: they and the matches they played count no more in its Standings and Matches (DRF-235, DRF-236), they leave its Games tab's games (DRF-121), their chat is the Game chat alone (no Pod tab, LOB-145), and its next draft's deck name and cover don't reach them, while renaming the deck or choosing its cover from Prepare deck in such a game's lobby changes that game's deck alone (DRF-105, DRF-166). (r16; owner, 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23)
- **DRF-040** Cancelling keeps source, settings and remaining members; starting again deals a fresh shuffle from the same source. One completed draft per pod. (r16)
- **DRF-041** Leaving after the draft withdraws your unaccepted challenges; when only one member is left the pod is destroyed. Destroy is confirmed and removes the pod for everyone. (r16)
- **DRF-042** Games already started from a pod survive any leave, cancel or destroy and stay in the normal Games list. (r16)

## Bot seats

- **DRF-296** Booster, Custom booster, Grid, Rochester, Rotisserie, Winston and Winchester take bot seats, default 0: seats the server fills with draft bots, up to the engine's player limit less one (7; Grid, Winston and Winchester 3): the creator may draft alone with bots (a solo draft). While the creator is the only member, with at least one bot and no invitation pending, setup says under its members "No one to play against: bots only draft, so this pod plays no games." (DRF-301). The creator adds them in setup with an "Add bot" button (an icon and text) beside the invite button (DRF-030), one seat a tap, greyed at the most with the reason on hold ("Booster takes at most 7 bots"); each bot row's ✕ removes a bot seat (holding it says "Remove Bot 2"; the bots left are numbered 1, 2… again). There is no Bots tile, and New pod has no Add bot (bots are added in setup). Sealed and Arena have no bots and no Add bot. (owner, 2026-11-21, 2026-11-22 and 2026-11-23)
- **DRF-297** Bot seats are named "Bot 1", "Bot 2"… with a robot avatar (a robot icon, never an initial): in setup listed after the members as "bot"; in a started pod among the members everywhere the draft shows players (header avatars and badges, waiting lines, pickers on boards, Picks, the seating ring). The Pods list's avatars and the Games tab (its members and standings) show only people. (owner, 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22)
- **DRF-298** The server makes a bot's move the moment a pack, grid, pile or turn reaches its seat, in the same step as the move that brought it there, so the draft never waits for a bot. A bot sees only what its seat may see and rates cards as the draft bot does (DRF-292); the server logs every bot move. (owner, 2026-11-21)
- **DRF-299** A bot burns, where a pick burns cards, the strongest cards left by their score without colour: what any player the pack goes to next would want most. (owner, 2026-11-21)
- **DRF-300** Cards a bot takes together (a Grid line, a Winston or Winchester pile) count best first, each half as much as the one before: one 99 beats two 50s, two 80s beat one 99. In Winston it takes the pile it looks at when worth 60 or more (the last pile with cards: 40, a blind card), else passes; it takes one it can't pass. (owner, 2026-11-21)
- **DRF-301** Bots only draft: their picks stay with the draft, they build no deck and play no games, and "Decks ready" comes when every person has submitted. (owner, 2026-11-21)

## Booster and Glimpse: how a draft runs

- **DRF-043** Seating is random (the server keeps it; the draft history shows it, DRF-210), but bot seats (DRF-296) are spread as evenly as possible between the people: the groups of bots between neighbouring people differ by at most one (two people and four bots always sit person, bot, bot, person, bot, bot; two people and three bots person, bot, person, bot, bot). Who sits where, which gaps hold the larger groups and the ring's rotation stay random; the bots sit in number order. Each round opens "open packs" packs per player; a round's packs all pass one way and the direction flips each round. (r16; owner, 2026-11-22)
- **DRF-044** A pick takes "keep" cards into your pool and removes "burn" cards from the draft; burned cards go to nobody. A confirmed pick cannot be undone. (r16)
- **DRF-045** A pack with keep cards or fewer left is given to its receiver automatically; with keep + burn + discard (DRF-046) or fewer you choose only the keeps and the rest go to nobody: up to burn of them burn and the others are thrown away; the draft history shows those thrown away as burns (DRF-207). (r42; owner 2026-11-22)
- **DRF-046** After a pick leaves "discard" cards or fewer in the pack, the rest are thrown away (shown in the picker's draft history as burns, DRF-207); 0 means never. (r16; owner 2026-11-22)
- **DRF-047** You open your next round's packs as soon as no pack of your opened rounds can come back to you for a pick. (r20)
- **DRF-048** You never see a next-round pack while a pack of your current round can still reach you; such packs wait hidden and send no "your pick". (r21)
- **DRF-049** Your queue is ordered by round, then fresher packs (fewer picks taken) first, then arrival; an arrival never replaces the pack you are looking at. (r22)
- **DRF-050** Cube cards are drawn without replacement for the whole draft from the pod's prepared Main (DRF-141); a Main too small for the settings blocks Start, and using every card exactly is fine. (r35)

## Arena

- **DRF-280** Arena: each player drafts on their own, at their own pace, and nothing passes between players. Every pick offers you a few cards; you take some of them and the rest of that offer is gone. When all your picks are done you go on to Build as in every draft.

A pick both apply to offers rares; the others commons.
- **DRF-282** Arena's "Mythic" tile: each card offered on a rare pick is a mythic rare instead with chance 1 in N, default 1/8, N 1–100 or Never.

- **DRF-284** "Reuse cards", a toggle tile for an Arena pod from a cube (default off, greyed for sets), works for each player on their own (owner, 2026-11-20): off, a card you picked is never offered to you again; on, any card may be. Cards offered to you but not taken stay in your own pool, and other players' picks never change what you are offered.
- **DRF-285** Each offer's cards are drawn evenly at random from the source's cards of its rarity, all different. - **DRF-286** Arena draws from the same sources as Booster: a cube's Main, a set (its own cards, a card once however many printings it has, never the five plain basic lands, snow-covered basics and Wastes as ordinary cards when the set's boosters hold them (owner, 2026-11-20), never two copies of one card in an offer, though it may come again later) or mixed sets, whose pack grid has a row per pick choosing that pick's set.
- **DRF-305** A set card with several printings in its set comes in an offer as one of them by how often the set's booster recipe puts each printing in a pack (Innistrad Remastered's retro-frame printings, one a pack, come far less often than the ordinary ones); a printing its boosters never hold never comes. Without a recipe, or when the recipe holds none of a card's printings, each printing is equally likely. (owner, 2026-11-21)
- **DRF-291** A set's special and bonus cards (bonus-sheet cards such as timeshifted reprints; owner, 2026-11-20) belong to the rarity of their other printings (the lowest), or to rare without any, and form that rarity's bonus bag in their set. Each card drawn for a rarity comes from its bonus bag with chance 20% × (bag cards ÷ the rarity's normal cards, at most 1), otherwise from the normal cards; a mythic upgrade draws on the mythic bags. In a cube such a card simply counts as that rarity.
- **DRF-287** Pod setup shows a summary under Arena's tiles: "Arena: 45 picks, 5 cards, take 1", then how many picks are rare, uncommon and common ("5 rare, 9 uncommon, 31 common picks"; "Rarity ignored" with Ignore rarity) and the cards each player ends with, for a cube its Reuse cards in words ("Reuse cards off: a card you pick is never offered to you again; other players' picks never change your offers"), then any rarity too short (DRF-285). A pod invitation reads the first line under the creator.
- **DRF-288** The Arena pick screen is Booster's (cards with holding to inspect and the desktop quick look, the keep tray and Confirm, no burns) with its first line "Pick 12 of 45 · Uncommon · take 1" (no rarity with Ignore rarity).
- **DRF-289** Arena's History icon opens "Draft history" with every pick of yours in order under "Pick 12 · Uncommon": the offer, your picks marked as kept; no seating ring and no "Returning packs only".
- **DRF-290** The server makes each player's offers from the draft's seeded random alone, keeps them and checks every pick; no other member ever sees your offers or picks. Your avatar badge counts the picks you have left, all of which you can make without waiting.
