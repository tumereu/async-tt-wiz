# History

The game timeline: what an event is and how it reads, how the other player's moves play back on your table (live and
when catching up), where playback stops, the event text chip, timeline mode, the game log and the playback settings.

## How it works

Everything that happens in a game is recorded as events, one for each point where someone acted. Your table plays the
other player's events back as animations, one at a time, whether you watch live or come back later to catch up; a
finger held on the table pauses what your view is waiting on, and the main button skips it. Catching up starts only when you tap the text in the
middle of the board, and it stops wherever you have to decide. A short text at the top says what just happened.
Timeline mode is a read-only viewer for looking back through the whole game, and the game log lists the same events as
lines of text. What a play shown, a combat hold or a
reveal looks like is described with the Stack, combat and choices; assumed answers and branches in async-play.md; undo
in undo.md.

## Events

- **HIS-092** Each history event is one acting point: a player's input (a cast or land play, a pass, an answer, a declaration) with everything that then happens by itself folded in (automatic answers, forced passes, their device's passes by response mode, resolutions, mana, untapping and drawing, steps passed, triggers placed, state-based actions), up to the next point where someone can act. (r48)
- **HIS-118** An assumed answer (HIS-096) always begins its own event and never shares it with a real input. (r48)
- **HIS-058** End turn (TRN-057) is your device passing your priority until the turn ends (or the other player does anything but pass in it, TRN-168): your passes fold into the events that reach them and never pause playback, stop or notify. The other player's passes assumed on the way are events of their own, and their playback stops at those it stops at. (r53)
- **HIS-101** Passes are implied: no "passed" line appears anywhere (timeline, event chip, Undo dialog, notifications), assumed or not, though passes stay in the game's record. A pass shows only as what it led to: "Storm Spark resolved" ("Ember Herald's ability resolved"), "Turn 3 → Bob" or the new step ("Combat"); one that led to none of these shows nowhere. (r61)
- **HIS-119** On the timeline and in the Undo dialog a pass is an event only when something resolved, when it began your own End turn ("You ended the turn"), or when it is your tap of the main button leaving your own main phase with an empty Stack ("You went to combat"; from your second main phase in Control, "You went to the end step"). (r61)
- **HIS-120** Your End turn and your tap leaving your own main phase are kept on the game's record with the event (what you pressed, on the press's first pass only), so they stay after a restart, on reopening the game and on another device; they show only to you. Events from before the record held them have none. (r61)

## Event texts and icons

- **HIS-061** Every event has a short text: hold an icon on the timeline. Texts name who acted first ("You", the other player's name) and end with what it led to. (r55)
- **HIS-091** Event texts name the actor: "You cast Storm Spark", "Bob played Mountain", "You activated Ember Herald's ability" (STK-046), "You attacked Bob with 2", "Bob blocked with 1"; a special action by what it did: "Bob unlocked Dusty Attic", "You turned Ember Mask face up", "Bob foretold a card", "Bob plotted Ember Rider", "Bob suspended Ember Comet". (r80)
- **HIS-243** A cast or an activation names what it targeted, after what was done: "Bob cast Storm Spark targeting your Ember Cub", "You activated Ember Herald's ability targeting Bob", "You cast Storm Spark targeting yourself"; several as "you and your Ember Cub", past three the first three and "and 2 more". (r80)
- **HIS-244** A target reads as it was when targeted, also after it left: on the battlefield or the Stack by its controller ("Bob's Ember Cub"), elsewhere by where it was ("Ember Bones in Bob's graveyard", "Ember Cub in exile"); one the viewer can't see as "Bob's face-down creature", "a card in Bob's hand" (HIS-062). (r80)
- **HIS-245** An attack names what it attacked, with how many: "You attacked Bob with 2", "Bob attacked you with 1 and your Ember Sage with 1" (a planeswalker or battle by its controller, as HIS-244). (r80)
- **HIS-121** Keyword abilities read by their keyword: "Bob cycled Shattered Reason", "You activated Ember Blade's equip ability". A keyword's ability or trigger resolving reads by its kind (STK-050): "Shattered Reason's cycling trigger resolved"; other abilities read "Ember Herald's ability resolved". (r76)
- **HIS-122** Table-only events read "Alice staged 2 attackers", "Bob arranged 1 block", "You answered early", "A result was suggested". (r76)
- **HIS-123** An answer you assumed reads, for you only, as theirs with "(assumed)": "Bob: no blocks (assumed)", "Bob kept (assumed)", and a stand-in bottom choice "Bob chose (assumed)", never naming the cards. They read it as their own, so nothing tells them it was assumed. (r76)
- **HIS-124** An event that reveals a secret choice (TBL-133: a cost "Reveal the creature type you chose") adds what was revealed after what was done, for both players: "Bob activated Ember Lineup's ability targeting Bob's Goblin Token · Bob revealed: Goblin". (r80)
- **HIS-234** An event that exiles cards face up from a library adds them after what was done, for both players: "Bre of Clan Stoutarm's ability resolved · Exiled from Bob's library: Plains, Mountain". (r78)
- **HIS-062** Texts never reveal what the viewer could not see at that event (hand, library, face-down, the other player's look); hidden cards read "a card" or "N cards", and a secret choice (TBL-133) is never named before it is revealed. (r76)
- **HIS-070** An event's icon is its input's: a cast = layers, a land = dashboard, an activation = a click, a pass = an arrow (a check when something resolved, skip-next for a new turn), attacks and blocks = swords, a keep = a check, a mulligan = shuffle, a concession = the outlined flag, results = the trophy, staging = the crosshair, an early answer = the note, the game's start = the flag, a new line after a rewind = the restore icon. (r49)
- **HIS-074** An event the app has no text or icon for reads "<name> acted" with the click icon instead of crashing (SYS-034). (r49)

## Playback: how moves animate

- **HIS-021** Playback advances one event at a time. An event that changes something you can see (a card moving, cast, played, resolving, dying or drawn; tapping, life, counters or damage; attacks and blocks; the other player's choices; a new turn) keeps its animation and then waits the playback delay: 0.33 s at Card move speed 1×, scaled by it (0.11 s at 3×, 1.32 s at 0.25×). (r74)
- **HIS-125** Instead of the playback delay, an event with a play shown (HIS-112) waits that show (shorter for a repeat), and an event that left an entry it put on the Stack there waits only for that entry's own animation (its move to the Stack and its arrows, STK-058). Nothing holds a card on the Stack for a fixed time. (r74)
- **HIS-104** In playback, passes, steps where nothing happened and declaring no attackers or no blockers go by at once (only the step indicator moves), unless your response mode is Control (TRN-059), where they keep the playback delay. The other player's staging and early answers take 0.12 s. (r61)
- **HIS-130** In Control, the other player's pass right after their own action still goes by at once (a resolve by hand after their cast, which a lower mode would have made inside that action's event, HIS-188). (r61)
- **HIS-102** With everything before them shown (your live game), the other player's passes show at once on the live table, never as playback; what they lead to (a spell resolving) animates as usual. (r52)
- **HIS-030** Playback and live opponent actions animate each change (cards fly, rotate, flip, fade; also between piles); attacks play back in the combat area (CMB-007). Take-backs animate backwards: an undo in one go on both tables (for the other player only as far as they had seen, HIS-108), a branch's take-back step by step (HIS-106). (r53)
- **HIS-031** Changes show a delta tag such as "+2" and a number flash for life, player counters, card P/T and counters (shown 1 s, then a 0.5 s fade; changes within 1.5 s add up), and a short highlight for face, control and step changes; a Room whose door was unlocked pulses as a card changing control does, and so does a card the other player's special action shown has landed in (HIS-237; owner, 2026-11-20). (r60)
- **HIS-131** Delta tags, flashes and highlights show for your own and the opponent's changes, live and in playback (catch-up playback too), also when a take-back goes back; never on jumps, on opening the game or in timeline mode. They come from the events shown (life, P/T, step and turn, face, a change of control, a library's shuffle). (r60)

## Pausing with a held finger

- **HIS-126** A finger touching down on the table (a play shown and a combat's target chip included) pauses whatever your view is waiting on at once, not only once it is known to be a hold: a play shown, a Stack entry's arrows growing (STK-058), a combat's hold, playback between events, live and in catch-up alike. Lifted before the hold's time (a tap, which acts as before) or moving on as a pan or a drag (which isn't a hold), it goes on; held, it stays paused however long it stays (and inspects, HIS-127). It goes on when lifted, with the time left. (r74; owner, 2026-11-26)
- **HIS-127** A finger held on a card also inspects it, and a pinned Inspect (TBL-131) keeps the pause until it is closed. While paused, a pause icon shows in the board's top-right corner, fading in and out quickly. (r74)
- **HIS-128** Only your view waits, never the game: while your view waits (a held finger, a play shown, a combat's hold) your phone makes none of its own steps, and the other player's phone goes on. (r74)
- **HIS-129** The held-finger pause doesn't touch what isn't timed for your view: a card already moving, the turn banner (TRN-074), a branch's take-back, the catch-up's centre text or a stop. Timeline mode's Auto keeps its own pause (HIS-017). (r74)

## Coming back to a game: catch-up

- **HIS-032** The server keeps, per game and account, your local head: the newest event shown to you (live or in playback) or added by you; another device opens the game at it. Everything shown to you (timeline, chips, result dialog, game list times, notification texts) comes from it and your next stop. (r55)
- **HIS-094** Catching up: coming back with the other player's events past your local head shows no dialog: your table as you left it, looking exactly like live play (no dimming, grey bars, label or other sign that you are catching up; no event strip, HIS-001), and in the board's centre a large text with a play icon, "<name> has made moves · Tap to watch", with no count or preview. (r60)
- **HIS-158** When what waits begins with a take-back of what you had seen, the centre text reads "<name> responded differently · Tap to watch" for a branch (HIS-106) and "<name> undid moves · Tap to watch" for their undo (HIS-108). At a branch's stop at the branch point it reads "<name> responded differently from here · Tap to continue". (r60)
- **HIS-159** A tap on the centre text, or anywhere on the table while it shows (the battlefields, the combat band, the cards and piles on them), starts the playback and the text goes at once. Your hand and the buttons keep their own taps, and holding a card still inspects it. There is no Play/Pause button. (r60)
- **HIS-160** Catch-up playback also looks like live play: the other player's actions animate as if they happened now (HIS-021, HIS-104), new events of theirs arriving meanwhile join it, and reaching the newest event you are live, with nothing announcing it. (r60)
- **HIS-161** The opening (mulligans) is never played back: the other player's opening declarations show at once, up to a decision of yours or the first turn, and the catch-up's pause starts after them unless you already answered a decision there. (r60)
- **HIS-033** Opening a game with the other player's events past your local head starts the catch-up paused with its centre text; its tap then plays them back, stopping at your decision points (HIS-095). Playback never skips ahead by itself (only the main button skips, TRN-165). When everything since is your own, the game opens live. (r59)
- **HIS-035** Leaving the app pauses playback at the shown point at once; coming back with the other player's events past your local head starts the catch-up paused with its centre text instead of rushing through them. A return where only chat or other non-event data changed, or only your own events arrived, stays live. (r59)
- **HIS-162** Opening or coming back to a game whose playback stands at a stop of yours (HIS-095: the next event is the other player's assumed answer where you decide) shows that stop, live, with no centre text, whatever of theirs follows it (nothing to watch before your decision). Unless a branch or the other player's undo took that stop back: then it opens as you left it, with the centre text, waiting for your tap (HIS-106, HIS-108). (r59)

## Playback stops

- **HIS-093** Catch-up playback stops at every point HIS-095 names, and at reveals (HIS-110), and waits there with no time limit. (r58)
- **HIS-095** Catch-up playback shows the other player's events one at a time and stops before an assumed answer about you where your current response mode stops (STK-036: Control at every point where you get priority, Normal at your pauses, Quick where you could respond; your decisions, assumed blocks and an assumed keep always): the same mode as live play's. (r76)
- **HIS-163** Playback judges events made earlier by your response mode now, not the one they recorded. Playback's quick actions show and change the mode as live play's do (TRN-059); changing it at a stop leaves that stop until you answer it, and the new mode applies from the next point on (STK-036). (r76)
- **HIS-164** Catch-up playback also stops at the other player's assumption that took the place of a move of yours (HIS-187), and right after a reveal until you dismiss its panel. Elsewhere, showing an assumed pass confirms it. (r76)
- **HIS-165** A tap on the table (a card, a pile or the empty table) while catch-up playback runs pauses it: it goes on to the next point where you get priority (where Control would stop) and stops there, live, with the centre text (HIS-094) back, whose tap resumes it. The pause also ends once nothing is left to play back, or when you went on in the game on another device of yours. (r76)
- **HIS-105** At a playback stop the table is live at that point, exactly like live play: quick actions, the main button (Resolve with something on the Stack, STK-029), casting and activating, with nothing saying it was assumed. Answering the same confirms it and playback goes on by itself (an answer that starts the same and goes on, such as passing and then the discard it leads to, confirms it and the rest is your next action); anything else is a branch (HIS-097). (r53)

## Event text chip

- **HIS-075** A short text of what just happened shows on the table's top row (up to 2 lines, ~420 dp wide) without blocking taps; Settings "Event text" turns it off (default on). (r52)
- **HIS-076** In timeline mode the chip shows every event shown, yours too, also when scrubbing. Live it shows the newest of the events just shown (also in playback) and stays quiet on open and return. No panel repeats what the table already shows you did. (r52)
- **HIS-077** The chip appears and changes at once and fades out in 450 ms after the longer of 2 s and the History playback pause (HIS-028), on its own clock even while paused; moving to another board drops it at once. (r31)
- **HIS-166** While the other player makes a choice (Automatum's choice, their blockers, their mulligan) the chip shows that instead, in the wait's words (TRN-066), even with Event text off: for a moment, then it fades even while the choice lasts (the wait band goes on saying it). Never on the chooser's own table, and not in timeline mode, where the chip shows only events. (r52)
- **HIS-167** While the other player is choosing, the chip's choice text shows again, flashing once in the warning colour, when something you try is refused (TBL-118). (r52)
- **HIS-168** An undo, yours or the other player's, shows no text at the top; your device only logs what it took back ("Undid: cast Storm Spark", "End turn" for an undone End turn, "went to combat" for a tap leaving a main phase; "Bob undid: …" for theirs). (r52, owner 2026-11-22, 2026-11-23)

## Timeline mode

- **HIS-001** In play mode, live and in catch-up playback, the bottom bar has no event strip: it holds only the controls, and its room goes to the board. Nothing past your local head (HIS-032) shows anywhere. (r55)
- **HIS-004** Table actions' "Timeline" entry (TRN-010) and a line of the game log (HIS-241) are the only ways into timeline mode; the Timeline entry opens it paused at the newest event shown to you, and the timeline ends there. (r80)
- **HIS-006** Live play, catch-up playback and timeline mode are three different things. Timeline mode starts only when you enter it, also during playback, which then waits paused. Opponent actions, hold-inspect and menu actions keep the table live, and so does the game loading again while the table is live: only coming back or opening starts the catch-up. (r53)
- **HIS-008** In timeline mode (and while a branch's take-back goes back, HIS-106) the top and bottom bars turn grey, the battlefield is dimmed, and the board centre shows a faint "Timeline mode" label with icon and, on its own line, the playback status "Playing" or "Paused" with its icon. Catch-up playback never looks like this. (r53)
- **HIS-007** In portrait the hand, piles and life sit directly above the bottom bar in play mode and above the timeline panel in timeline mode; the two cross-fade in about 200 ms. The timeline panel is taller there (its strip above Previous / Auto / Next), so entering timeline mode moves them up by the strip's height and leaving moves them back. (r60)
- **HIS-169** In landscape your life, piles and hand sit in the bottom bar itself (TBL-009), and the timeline panel's strip and Previous / Auto / Next take the controls' fixed-width place at its right end, so nothing moves on entering or leaving timeline mode. (r60)
- **HIS-010** In the phone layout (the desktop table: WEB-021) the timeline panel has the strip with a playback settings gear at its end, and below it Previous, the Auto button and Next. Previous/Next are plain borderless arrows, Next at the last event too. There is no game tree button. (r61)
- **HIS-005** On the strip each event is a 20 dp icon at a 24 dp pitch, coloured by who made it (your teal, the opponent's red, system grey). There is one line of play: no branch lines or markers. (r57)
- **HIS-170** Where the turn or step changed between two events, a thin vertical line sits in the gap between them with a small label at its top right, "T3 · Combat" ("Opening" before the first turn), in the colour of the player whose turn it is; a new turn's line is stronger. A turn where nothing happened shows only as such a line. (r57)
- **HIS-171** When several turn or step changes fall between two events only the latest shows, and where labels would overlap the newer keeps its label (the lines all show). (r57)
- **HIS-011** Swiping the strip or tapping an event moves the review cursor to that exact event; holding an event shows its text without moving the cursor. With a mouse in the desktop layout, resting the pointer on an event half a second (WEB-028) or a right-click on it shows the same text, until the pointer leaves it or a button is pressed; holding it with the mouse works as a finger's hold. (r33)
- **HIS-012** Previous and Next step one event along the main line, from the game's first event (HIS-111) up to your local head. The strip shows that line. (r58)
- **HIS-111** The timeline (timeline mode's strip, Previous and playback) and the Undo dialog's strip reach back to the game's first event ("The game began", with the flag) however long the game is. Nothing limits how many events back you can look or undo. (r58)
- **HIS-172** Older events load from the server as you go back: a small spinner at the strip's left end while they come, the board staying at the last point shown until the next one's events are in. Play never waits for them. (r58)
- **HIS-013** The Auto button reads "Auto: off" (green play icon) or "Auto: on" (orange pause icon), or at the last event "Exit timeline" (HIS-015); tapping toggles automatic playback. Timeline mode is the read-only history viewer: it never stops for your decisions and nothing in it acts on the game. (r53)
- **HIS-023** Starting Auto takes its first step right away; later steps keep the pause. (r31)
- **HIS-009** When playback starts or stops (by hand or at the end), a large play or pause sign flashes in the board's top-right corner. (r54)
- **HIS-015** Navigating to the last event by hand stays in timeline mode, and there (also as timeline mode opens, at the newest event) the Auto button reads "Exit timeline" with a close icon: one tap leaves timeline mode at once, to where you were (HIS-018). Moving back from the last event makes it Auto again. (r61)
- **HIS-173** Automatic playback reaching the end returns to play mode by itself, its button still "Auto: on" and its animation still playing. (r61)
- **HIS-016** In timeline mode double tap does nothing (no error), a one-finger drag on empty table pans, and card menus do not act on the game. (r45)
- **HIS-017** Hold-inspect works on every face-up card in timeline mode too, and there holding a card stops Auto, which stays off after the release. (r61)
- **HIS-018** "Return to current" in the top bar, and Back, leave timeline mode to where you were: live play, or the catch-up paused with its centre text (HIS-094). (r53)
- **HIS-019** Leaving timeline mode never asks a question; the timeline has no branching action (a branch happens only when your real answer differs from an assumed one, HIS-097). (r48)

## The game log

- **HIS-239** Table actions' "Game log" (TRN-010) opens the game log over the table, on the phone and the desktop: the timeline's events (HIS-005, HIS-111) and the log's own combat damage lines (HIS-247), one line each in tight rows (about 24 dp, so many fit) with its icon in its author's colour and its text (HIS-061), oldest at the top and newest at the bottom, where it opens. (r80, owner 2026-11-21, 2026-11-22)
- **HIS-242** In the game log the timeline's turn and step labels ("T3 · Combat", HIS-170) sit between the lines where the turn or step changed, in the colour of the player whose turn it is; new events join at the bottom while it is open. (r80)
- **HIS-240** In a game log line each player's name ("You", "Bob") and each card it names show in that player's colour (yours green, the other player's red, as the Stack's target names, STK-133): a card cast, played or activated by who did it, what resolved by its controller, cards exiled from a library by its owner, a target in its words ("Bob's Ember Cub") by whose it is (HIS-244); "a card" and a hidden target stay plain. (r80)
- **HIS-241** Tapping a game log line closes the log and opens timeline mode paused at that event; scrolling to the log's top loads older events from the server with a small spinner, back to "The game began" (HIS-172). (r80)
- **HIS-246** Under a game log line, smaller indented lines say what it caused, one line per kind (and where to) in the order things happened: triggers put on the Stack ("Ember Herald's ability triggered"), permanents leaving the battlefield ("Ember Cub died", a sacrifice too; "Ember Cub was exiled", "Ember Cub returned to Bob's hand", "Ember Cub was put into Bob's library"), life changes with the total after ("Bob lost 3 life (17)", "You gained 2 life (22)", "You paid 2 life (18)"), tokens created ("You created 2 Goblin tokens"), cards drawn by a spell or ability, discarded and milled ("Bob discarded Ember Cub", "Bob milled Mountain and Storm Spark"), and counters put on or removed with the count after ("Ember Cub got a +1/+1 counter (2)"; several: "Ember Cub (2) and Ember Sage (1) got a +1/+1 counter"). Names show in their players' colours (HIS-240); past three names, the first three and "and 2 more". A tap on one acts as its line's. (owner 2026-11-22)
- **HIS-247** Where combat damage was dealt, the log has a line of its own, "Combat damage" ("First-strike damage" in that step), with the swords icon in grey and the deaths and life changes (and triggers) it caused under it: right after the line of the event it came in ("Bob: no blocks", whose own indented lines are what came before the damage), or between the lines when the timeline doesn't show that event. It is only in the log, not on the timeline or in the Undo dialog; tapping it opens timeline mode at the event it came in, or when the timeline doesn't show that one, at the next event on the timeline (the one before while none follows). (owner 2026-11-22)
- **HIS-248** Triggers put on the Stack in events the log has no line for (an upkeep or draw-step trigger) show as indented lines under the next line's turn and step label (under the line before when the next line has no label), once that next line is there. (owner 2026-11-22)
- **HIS-249** The indented lines never name what the viewer couldn't see (HIS-062): the other player's draws are a count ("Bob drew 2 cards"), your own are named ("You drew Mountain and Ember Cub"); discarded and milled cards are public and named to both; a face-down creature leaving for where it stays hidden reads "Bob's face-down creature returned to Bob's hand". (owner 2026-11-22)
- **HIS-250** No indented line for the draw step's draw, opening hands and mulligans, tapping, damage that didn't destroy, or a cycled card's discard (its line says it); a P/T change shows only as counters, never as a line of its own. (owner 2026-11-22)

## Playback settings

- **HIS-028** Playback settings live in Settings under "Timeline playback" and in a dialog from the timeline's gear: "History playback pause", "Rewind pause" and Card move speed; Settings' group also has "Show plays and combat" (HIS-112, HIS-113), the gear's dialog doesn't. There are no auto-rewind switches. (r58)
- **HIS-174** The History playback pause paces only timeline mode's Auto and the event chip's fade (HIS-077); live and catch-up playback use the playback delay (HIS-021). (r61)
- **HIS-022** The "Rewind pause", 0.1 s by default, adjustable from 0.02 s to 2 s, paces a take-back after a branch or a rewind (HIS-106, HIS-099); there is no rewinding in timeline mode. (r49)
- **LOB-240** The History playback pause is 1 s by default, adjustable from 0.1 s to 10 s. (r74)
- **LOB-082** "Show plays and combat" is 1.2 s by default, adjustable from 0 to 3 s in 0.1 s steps (shown as "<n> s each"); 0 reads "Off": no play shows and no combat holds (HIS-112, HIS-113). It is how long a play shown holds in the middle, not scaled by Card move speed (TBL-038). (r74)
