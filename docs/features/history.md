# History

The game timeline: the event strip, timeline mode and playback, catching up on what happened (the paused catch-up, playback stops, assumed answers, branches when an answer differs), lines of play, undo and undo requests, event texts and icons, and how events are grouped by turn.

### Play-mode event strip

- **HIS-001** In play mode the bottom bar shows a read-only strip of the latest events up to the last one shown to you (your local head, HIS-032), laid out like the timeline: the newest event is centred in a square highlight; playback shows it too (HIS-013). Nothing past your local head shows anywhere (I-D56 §7). (r51)
- **HIS-002** The strip can be panned by hand to look back; it snaps back to the newest event shown to you whenever a new one is shown, with no idle-time snap-back. Panning never changes the board. (r48)
- **HIS-003** Tapping an event on the strip shows its text in a small dark popup that fades after about 1.6 s; holding keeps it shown. No ripple on the strip. (r17)
- **HIS-004** A history icon button at the end of the strip enters timeline mode; Table actions has a "Timeline" entry that does the same. Both open timeline mode paused at the newest event shown to you; the timeline ends there. (r48)
- **HIS-005** Each event is a 20 dp icon at a 24 dp pitch, coloured by who made it (your teal, the opponent's red, system grey). A faint vertical line separates turns. There is one line of play and no branch markers (I-D56 §8). (r48)

### Timeline mode

- **HIS-006** On engine state (I-D56 §7) coming back with the other player's events past your local head starts the catch-up (HIS-094): playback paused until you tap Play, then stopping at your decision points (HIS-095); only your own events since open live. Otherwise timeline mode starts only when you enter it; opponent actions, hold-inspect and menu actions keep the table live. (r52)
- **HIS-007** Timeline mode and play mode use the same bottom area height and cross-fade in about 200 ms, so the board never jumps; the hand, piles and life sit unchanged directly above the timeline panel. (r45)
- **HIS-008** In timeline mode the top and bottom bars turn grey, the battlefield is dimmed, and the board centre shows a faint "Timeline mode" label with icon and, on its own line, the playback status "Playing" or "Paused" with its icon. (r49)
- **HIS-009** When playback starts or stops (by hand, by auto-pause or at the end), a large play or pause sign flashes in the board's top-right corner. (r49)
- **HIS-010** The timeline panel has the strip with a playback settings gear at its end, and below it Previous, the Auto button and Next. Previous/Next are plain borderless arrows. There is no game tree button. (r48)
- **HIS-011** Swiping the strip or tapping an event moves the review cursor to that exact event; holding an event shows its text without moving the cursor. (r33)
- **HIS-012** Previous and Next step one event along the main line, up to your local head. The strip shows that line. (r48)
- **HIS-013** The Auto button reads "Auto: off" (green play icon) or "Auto: on" (orange pause icon); tapping toggles automatic playback. There is no "Hold to branch" line: the timeline is view-only. Playing back the other player's actions (HIS-095) has the event strip at the bottom and, where Auto is, a play/pause button ("Pause" while playing, "Play" once paused and while catching up (HIS-094), "Pausing" until it stops) with "Hold: response mode" under it: a tap pauses or plays again; pausing goes on to the next point where Full control would stop for you (a point where you could have acted), even with no decision waiting, and stops there, the table live so you may act or play on; holding it opens "Response mode" with Full control, Normal and Auto-skip and what each means (TRN-059). (r52)
- **HIS-015** Navigating to the last event by hand stays in timeline mode; only automatic playback reaching the end returns to play mode, even if the last event would auto-pause, and its animation still plays. On engine state playback stops at your decision points (HIS-095), where the table is live. (r48)
- **HIS-016** In timeline mode double tap does nothing (no error), a one-finger drag on empty table pans, and card menus do not act on the game. (r45)
- **HIS-017** Hold-inspect works on every face-up card in timeline mode too, and holding a card pauses playback. (r8)
- **HIS-018** "Return to current" in the top bar leaves timeline mode for the live game; while you are behind (events past your local head, HIS-032) it and Back play on quickly to your next decision point, never past it. (r48)
- **HIS-019** Leaving timeline mode never asks a question; the timeline has no branching action (I-D56 §8: a branch happens only when your real answer differs from an assumed one, HIS-097). (r48)
- **HIS-020** A pending win/loss/draw suggestion never blocks the timeline. It locks play only once the board shown to you (your local head) includes it (TRN-049). (r48)

### Playback

- **HIS-021** Playback advances one event at a time. An event that changes something you can see (a card moving, cast, played, resolving, dying or drawn; tapping, life, counters or damage; attacks and blocks; the other player's choices; a new turn) keeps its animation and then waits the playback delay, 0.33 s at Card move speed 1× and scaled by it (TBL-038: 0.11 s at 3×, 1.32 s at 0.25×); passes, steps where nothing happened and a combat with no attackers (declaring no attackers or no blockers) go by at once (only the step indicator moves) unless your response mode is Full control, where they keep the delay; the other player's staging, card places and early answers take 0.12 s. The History playback pause (HIS-028) paces timeline mode's Auto. An attack plays back in the combat area in the middle of the table, as in live combat (CMB-007). (r52)
- **HIS-022** The "Rewind pause", 0.1 s by default, adjustable from 0.02 s to 2 s, paces a take-back after a branch or a rewind (HIS-097, HIS-099); there is no rewinding in timeline mode. (r49)
- **HIS-023** Starting Auto takes its first step right away; later steps keep the pause. (r31)
- **HIS-024** Auto stops after an opponent's event that matches an enabled auto-pause trigger, after that event's animation. Your own actions never auto-pause. On engine state a decision stop (HIS-095) always stops playback, with no time limit, whatever the switches say. (r48)
- **HIS-025** Auto-pause triggers and defaults: Added to Stack on, Removed from Stack off, Reveal on, Your life hit off, Your cards hit on, Move passes to you on, Attackers declared off, Opponent plays a land off, Opponent puts a nonland card onto the battlefield from hand off. (r46)
- **HIS-093** On engine state playback stops at every decision point of yours by your response mode (HIS-095), whatever the auto-pause switches say; it waits there with no time limit, and holding Auto does nothing more (no branching from the timeline). (r48)
- **HIS-094** Catching up (I-D56 §7, async-play §29.1): coming back with the other player's events past your local head shows no dialog: your table where you left it, with playback paused, the event strip and Play at the bottom where the play/pause button is (HIS-013: a tap starts the playback, holding it opens the response mode). Nothing shows a count or preview of what is coming. The opening (mulligans) is never played back: the other player's opening declarations show at once, in one board, up to a decision of yours or the first turn; the catch-up pause starts past them, unless you already answered a decision there. (r52)
- **HIS-095** Playback on engine state shows the other player's events one at a time (HIS-021), and stops before an assumed answer about you wherever your response mode stops (Full control: every assumed pass; Normal: your stops and pauses; Auto-skip: those where you could respond; assumed blocks and keeps always stop), or where Full control would stop once you paused it (HIS-013). There the table is live at that point and looks like any point where you may act (your quick actions, step arrow or Resolve), with nothing saying it was assumed: answering the same confirms it and playback goes on (an answer that starts with the same and goes on, such as passing and then the discard it leads to, confirms it and the rest is your next action after it); anything else is a branch (HIS-097). Elsewhere showing an assumed pass confirms it. With everything before them shown (your live game), the other player's passes arriving show at once on the live table (what they lead to, such as a spell resolving, animates as usual), never as playback. (r52)
- **HIS-096** Assumed answers (I-D56 §3, §4): at the other player's priority points the device assumes their pass ("Assume resolves"), their blocks ("Assume blockers") or their opening keep ("Assume keep") so the game can go on while they are away, never at their own main phase. Their pauses are assumed at once while they are away, and waited for while they are online (green dot) unless "Always assume resolves" is on; a wait that began while they were online turns async 60 s after they were last online. Any other decision of theirs is a real wait. An assumption whose outcome would show you a hidden card or a random result is not made (TRN-067). (r48)
- **HIS-097** Branch (I-D56 §8): when the other player's real answer differs from what was assumed, the game goes on from their answer and the line after it is discarded: they never see it; your table goes back with the rewind animation and a note "<name> responded — your later actions were taken back" ("blocked differently", "took a mulligan"). A new line starts (HIS-042). There is no other way to branch. (r48)
- **HIS-098** Free undo (I-D56 §10): free when everything after the point is your own (real inputs or assumptions not yet confirmed) or the other player's table-only events the undo rule lets stand, and none of it showed you something new: a card you couldn't see before (your draw, look, scry, search, a reveal), a random result, or a wait for the other player's choice from a source you can't see. (r48)
- **HIS-099** Undo dialog and requests (I-D56 §10): holding Undo, or an Undo that isn't free, opens "Undo": the actions shown to you, newest first (no passes, HIS-091; your End turn that began with a pass is one entry, "You ended the turn"), to pick the point to go back to, with a small view of the board at that point below them, then Undo (free) or, when the other player must approve, an optional message and "Ask <name>". They get "<name> asks to undo" with the message, where the game goes back to as far as they have seen ("and actions you haven't seen yet are taken back" when past that) with a small view of that board (their own newest board when the point is past what they have seen), and Deny / Accept; it never says how many events go. Answering it is their move: the game is listed as theirs and they get a "Your move" Game activity notification (LOB-072), until they answer or the requester cancels. While a request is open neither player acts (the wait says so, TRN-066; the requester may Cancel request); looking and playback go on; a concession still ends the game. A rewind only deletes the events after the point (HIS-086); a player who had seen past it has their board go back the way it came (HIS-030), with "<name> undid: <action>" fading at the top (HIS-076). (r52)
- **HIS-090** "Your life hit" is an opponent's change to your life or your player counters; "Your cards hit" is an opponent's action on a card you own or control (also its combat copies and Stack entries) or one giving you a card. Each device's earlier "Your life / cards hit" choice carries over to "Your cards hit". (r36)
- **HIS-026** A hand-to-battlefield move never counts as a Reveal. (r13)
- **HIS-027** "Move passes to you" pauses only if the next event is not your own. Status changes never auto-pause; opening a graveyard/exile panel is not a reveal. (r17)
- **HIS-028** Playback settings live in Settings under "Timeline playback" and in a dialog from the timeline's gear: the auto-pause switches ("Stop Auto after an opponent's matching action"), "History playback pause", "Rewind pause" and Card move speed. There are no auto-rewind switches. (r48)
- **HIS-030** Playback and live opponent actions animate each change (cards fly, rotate, flip, fade; moves between piles fly too). Rewinds and take-backs animate backwards; an undo (free or approved) plays back quickly on both players' tables, for the other player only as far as they had seen: each step of the undone actions in reverse, the cards flying back the way they came (a cast spell from the battlefield through the Stack to the hand) at the card move speed with no playback pause, while "Undid: <action>" fades at the top (HIS-076). (r52)
- **HIS-031** Changes show a delta tag and a number flash (about 1.5 s fade; changes in that window add up) for life, player counters, card P/T and counters, and a highlight for face and step changes; for your own and the opponent's changes, live and in playback, not on jumps. On engine state they come from the events shown (life, P/T, step and turn, face, a change of control, a library's shuffle), also when a take-back goes back; not on opening the game or in timeline mode. (r49)

### Opening and returning to a game

- **HIS-032** On engine state the server keeps, per game and account, your local head: the newest event shown to you (live or in playback) or added by you; a second device opens at it. Everything shown to you (strip, timeline, chips, result panel, game list times, notification texts) comes from it and your next stop (I-D56 §7); whose move it is follows TRN-058. (r50)
- **HIS-033** Opening a game with the other player's events past your local head starts with playback paused (HIS-094), with no count or preview of what is coming; Play then plays them back, stopping at your decision points (HIS-095). Playback never skips ahead (the opening shows at once, HIS-094). When everything since is your own, the game opens live. (r52)
- **HIS-034** A new device opens a game at your local head kept on the server (HIS-032). (r48)
- **HIS-035** Leaving the app pauses at the shown point at once; coming back with the other player's events past your local head starts with playback paused (HIS-094) instead of rushing through them. A return where only chat or other non-event data changed, or only your own events arrived, stays live. (r52)

### Lines of play

- **HIS-042** Every line of play starts by reshuffling the unknown parts of both libraries (shown nowhere): the game's start, every branch (HIS-097) and every rewind, free undo included (HIS-099). A card whose place someone knows (put on top or bottom by a known action, looked at or revealed where it lies, a kept-revealed top) keeps its place. Random results (shuffles, coin flips, dice, random choices) are worked out from the game's secret and the line, so the same input on a line gives the same result and a new line gives new results (I-D56 §9). (r48)
- **HIS-043** There is no branch archiving and no "Make current" action anywhere; lines left by a branch are kept for problem reports and shown to nobody. (r48)

### Events and turns

- **HIS-050** The timeline groups events by turn (a faint line between turns, HIS-005); each event is one acting point (HIS-092), and playback and Previous/Next go one event at a time. (r49)
- **HIS-058** End turn (TRN-057) is your device passing your priority until the turn ends: your passes fold into the events that reach them (HIS-092) and never pause playback, stop or notify; the other player's passes assumed on the way are events of their own (HIS-092), and the other player's playback stops at those their mode stops at (HIS-095). (r49)
- **HIS-092** History events are natural points where a player can act: on engine state each event is one acting point: the player's input (a cast or land play, a pass, an answer, a declaration) and everything that then happens by itself (engine-automatic answers, forced passes, the player's own device's passes by their response mode, resolutions, mana made and spent, untapping and drawing, steps passed on the way, triggers placed, state-based actions) folded into it, up to the next point where someone can act. An assumed answer (HIS-096) always begins its own event; assumed and real inputs never share one. (r48)

### Event texts

- **HIS-061** Every event has a short text: hold an icon on the strip or timeline. Texts name who acted first ("You", the other player's name) and end with what it led to (HIS-091). (r49)
- **HIS-062** Texts never reveal what the viewer could not see at that event (hand, library, face-down, the other player's look); hidden cards read "a card" or "N cards". (r27)
- **HIS-091** On engine state an answer you assumed for the other player reads, for you only, with them as its actor and "(assumed)": "Bob: no blocks (assumed)", "Bob kept (assumed)" (the strip, the event chip and the Undo dialog's list, HIS-099); the player it was assumed about reads it as their own answer ("You kept"), the same as if they had made it: nothing anywhere tells them whether the other player assumed or waited. Other events read plainly ("You cast Storm Spark", "Bob played Mountain", "You activated Ember Herald's ability" (an ability is named as its source's ability, STK-046), "You attacked with 2", "Bob blocked with 1"). Passes are implied: no "passed" line appears anywhere (the strip, the timeline, the event chip, the Undo dialog, notifications), assumed or not, though passes stay in the game's record; a pass shows only as what it led to, "Storm Spark resolved" ("Ember Herald's ability resolved"), "Turn 3 → Bob" or the new step ("Combat"), and one that led to none of these shows nowhere, so a turn where nothing happened shows only its step and turn changes. Table-only events read "Alice staged 2 attackers", "Bob arranged 1 block", "You answered early", "You moved a card", "A result was suggested". Hidden cards read "a card" (HIS-062). (r52)

### Event icons

- **HIS-070** An event's icon is its input's: a cast = layers, a land = dashboard, an activation = a click, a pass = an arrow (a check when something resolved, skip-next for a new turn), attacks and blocks = swords, a keep = a check, a mulligan = shuffle, a concession = the outlined flag, results = the trophy, staging = the crosshair, an early answer = the note, a card moved on the battlefield = the move icon, the game's start = the flag, a new line after a rewind = the restore icon. (r49)
- **HIS-074** An event the app has no text or icon for reads "<name> acted" with the click icon instead of crashing (SYS-034). (r49)

### Event text chip

- **HIS-075** A short text of what just happened shows on the table's top row (up to 2 lines, max ~420 dp wide), without blocking taps on the controls below. Settings "Event text" turns it off; default on. While the other player makes a choice (nobody holds priority: Automatum's choice while something resolves or a trigger's targets and modes, STK-030; their blockers; their mulligan) it shows that instead, in the wait's words (TRN-066: "Bob is choosing · Ember Divination", "Bob is declaring blockers", "Bob is deciding on a mulligan"), also with Event text off, as it is a status, not an event; it doesn't fade while the choice is open, flashes once in the warning colour when something you try meanwhile is refused (TBL-118), and once the choice is made the text is the events' again. The deciding player's own table never says it; timeline mode shows only the events (HIS-076). (r52)
- **HIS-076** In timeline mode it shows for every event shown, yours too, also when scrubbing; live it shows the newest event among those just shown, yours as well as the other player's (also in playback), fading like the other player's, and stays quiet on open and return; while the other player's choice is open it shows that instead (HIS-075). An undo says what it took back, also with Event text off: "Undid: cast Storm Spark" for the player who undid, "Alice undid: cast Storm Spark" for the other player when they had seen it ("End turn" for an undone End turn). No panel says what you just did when the table already shows it (the spell on the Stack, the card on the battlefield). (r52)
- **HIS-077** The chip appears and changes at once and fades out in 450 ms after the longer of 2 s and the playback pause, on its own clock even while paused; moving to another board drops it at once. (r31)

### Undo

- **HIS-083** The Undo button sits at the left end of the bottom bar: tap = Undo (HIS-084); hold = the Undo dialog to pick any earlier point (HIS-099), with a small haptic tick. There is no Redo. (r48)
- **HIS-084** On engine state Undo goes back to just before your latest own meaningful action (your later assumed answers go with it, and so do all passes after it: passes are implied (HIS-091), so a pass is never what Undo goes back to, neither your Next or Resolve taps nor the passes your device made for you by your response mode; an End turn is one action, undone as a whole): at once when it is free (HIS-098), else it opens the Undo dialog with that point chosen, to ask the other player (HIS-099). You land where you acted, and your device passes nothing for you there until you act again; Undo again goes back past your action before. There is no limit on how many events go back. (r52)
- **HIS-085** One acting point (a cast with its choices, a declaration with its payment) undoes as one. (r48)
- **HIS-086** Undo only deletes the events after the point: the game is exactly as it was there; nothing is added for the undo itself, so no timeline or strip event ever says the game went back (the new line's reshuffle, HIS-042, happens with the next event). (r51)
- **HIS-087** Undo is free only if nothing after the point is the other player's input (their table-only events stay when they touched nothing the undone events changed, SYS-058), none of your assumptions there has been confirmed yet (the other player was shown it), and nothing after the point showed you something new (HIS-098); otherwise it needs the other player's approval (HIS-099). (r49)
- **HIS-089** Undo never changes a line the other player is on without their approval. A refused undo says why in plain words; an undo with nothing to undo shakes the button. (r48)
