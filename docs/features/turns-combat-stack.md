# Turns, combat and Stack

Turn and step tracking, whose move it is, the top and bottom game bars, life, game start and result suggestions (`TRN`), the combat area (`CMB`) and the Stack (`STK`).

### Turn structure

- **TRN-001** A turn has 12 positions: Untap, Upkeep, Draw, Main 1, Beginning of combat, Declare attackers, Declare blockers, Combat damage, End of combat, Main 2, End step, Cleanup. Automatum moves the turn through them (TRN-057) and adds a First-strike damage step when a combat needs one (TRN-056). (r37)
- **TRN-056** A combat in which a first or double striker fights has two combat damage steps (CR 510.4): First-strike damage (a bolt icon, short name "1st strike"), then Combat damage. The step button shows it like any step; the phase dialog shows its square between Blockers and Damage from when the combat reaches it until the combat ends. (r46)
- **TRN-057** The main button moves the turn on for you only: a tap passes your priority (or declares what the combat lanes hold); holding it is End turn, your device passing your priority until the turn ends, attacking with nothing. The other player's points are assumed (HIS-096), automatic or waited for, never passed for them. Each step's own actions happen by themselves; nothing else changes the step. (r60)
- **TRN-060** When the game comes back to the same position with only passes nobody could avoid in between (CR 104.4b; holding only cards that can't be played then, such as lands while something is on the Stack, doesn't count as being able to act), the player holding priority is asked "The game is in a loop of actions nobody can stop, which makes it a draw (CR 104.4b). End it as a draw, or continue?" with Continue and Draw; Draw makes the game a draw (TRN-065). Abilities that keep triggering with nobody getting priority stop after 200 rounds with the same choice, "Possible loop: …". (r48)
- **TRN-002** Turn numbers count both players' turns, so turn 1 is only the starting player's first turn. Next step from Cleanup, or End turn, passes the turn to the other player's Untap. (r33)
- **TRN-052** A turn change Automatum makes names whose turn begins, so an extra turn goes to the same player again (turn number up by one, at Untap); the event that begins it reads "Turn N → <name>" ("Turn N → you" for that player, HIS-091). (r53)
- **TRN-004** While the other player must act, the main button (TRN-063) holds: nothing where nothing of yours follows before their next real action (their own main phase with an empty Stack, their declaration of attackers, their choices and, before your own opening is done, their mulligan); "Assume resolves" (something on the Stack) or "Assume pass" (an empty Stack: nothing to resolve) where they hold priority anywhere else and your phone doesn't assume their pass by itself (HIS-096, TRN-070); "Assume no blockers" / "Assume blockers" while they declare blockers (CMB-040; nothing where their blocks can't be assumed); "Assume keep" while they decide on their opening once your own is done (TRN-070); a greyed Assume where assuming would show you hidden cards or a random result (TRN-071); and "Cancel request" while your own undo request is open (HIS-100). Where it holds nothing, its place shows a quiet status in plain words instead of a button: grey words, nothing to tap, so the pause doesn't read as lag: "Bob's answer…" (they may respond or act), "Bob is choosing…", "Bob is blocking…", "Bob's mulligan…", "Bob's move…" (their main phase or their attack); none while your own events are on their way or an undo request or a result suggestion is answered in its dialog. (r76)

### Top bar

- **TRN-005** The top bar's first line reads "Your turn · N" or "<name>'s turn · N" in the turn player's colour; only the name shortens on a narrow screen. (r11)
- **TRN-006** Under it, the move pill: a solid green "Your move" when it is your move (TRN-058), otherwise an outlined "Waiting · <name>", also while catching up (HIS-094). Tapping it does nothing: the move isn't passed by hand. (r53)
- **TRN-007** In the phone layout the step button in the top bar is a square in the current phase's colour with the step's icon and its short name, as in the phase dialog (TRN-008), with no border colour or turn number. It flashes on a step change and opens the phase dialog. (r35)
- **TRN-008** The phase dialog is a real dialog titled with the step and its phase ("Combat · Declare attackers"), then "Your turn" / "<name>'s turn"; phase labels sit above rows of step squares, the current one outlined in the turn player's colour. Square names fit on one line on a phone: Untap, Upkeep, Draw · Main 1 · Begin, Attackers, Blockers, Damage, End · Main 2 · End, Cleanup ("1st strike" while a combat has it, TRN-056). (r37)
- **TRN-009** The phase dialog only shows where the turn is: its step squares do nothing and it has no buttons; the game moves on only with the main button (TRN-063). (r47)
- **TRN-010** The top bar's Table actions button opens a dialog with Timeline (the only way into timeline mode, HIS-004), Suggest game end (or Accept result while the opponent's suggestion waits), Concede (TRN-064), Send problem report (Settings' "Report a problem" with its optional description, sent without leaving the game) and Game settings (LOB-135), each an icon and its name. Nothing in it changes the board by hand (TBL-119). (r58)

### Whose turn and whose move

- **TRN-012** Whose TURN shows on the battlefields: only the turn player's battlefield is tinted (your own green-ish, the opponent's red-ish, the land band a little darker); the other battlefield is neutral grey. (r11)
- **TRN-013** Whose MOVE shows on the bars: the top and bottom bars take the light colour of the player whose move it is (TRN-058), never part of an event. In timeline mode both bars are grey. (r50)
- **TRN-074** Turn banner: at a turn change "Your turn" or "<name>'s turn" shows at the screen's centre in the turn player's colour and fades out in about 1.2 s, taking no touches, holding nothing and waiting for nothing but a play shown before it (HIS-112), live and in catch-up playback. It and the plays shown never overlap: a play that went on the Stack before the turn change shows first, one after it waits for the banner. Never in timeline mode, a take-back or on opening a game. (r61)
- **TRN-075** Your move cue: when the move passes to you from the other player (you may act again and their real input came since, or playback stops at their assumption about you, TRN-063), your phone gives one haptic tick, plays your "Your move" sound (LOB-146; none at Off) and the main button pulses once in your colour, once per passing, not repeating. Never for your own chain of steps, in playback only when you actually stop at a decision (not a stop your own pause made), not when you've just acted yourself (their answer within 1.5 s of your own action), not in timeline mode or on opening a game; while a play, a combat or a reveal holds your view it comes when that is done. (r64)
- **TRN-058** The move is the player the game really waits for (the record "Your move" notifications use, LOB-073). While one player plays on assumptions about the other, the acting player has it, even while the other's playback stops at an assumption they may answer. An open undo request (HIS-100) or result suggestion (TRN-047) is the answerer's move; a result the rules gave is the move of whoever hasn't pressed OK on it (TRN-065). When nobody is waited for, the game list says "Move not available". The move changes only where the game really waits: at a point on the way, where the next step follows by itself (a pass your or their own mode makes, an assumption your phone makes, the other phone's events still arriving one at a time), it stays as it was. The move passing to you has a cue (TRN-075). (r61)

### Bottom bar and quick actions

- **TRN-015** The bottom bar shows your toggles (TRN-059) on either player's turn and the main button with what it holds now (TRN-063, TRN-004); where it holds nothing its place shows the quiet waiting status while you wait on the other player (TRN-004) and otherwise stays empty, in your own turn too. During your opening your quick actions are Mulligan and Keep (TRN-062). (r60)
- **TRN-016** Quick actions are equal-width buttons in the popup style (icon plus small black label, 48dp tall) sharing the width left of the main button (TRN-017) equally, with press feedback. A greyed quick action stays in place. (r56)
- **TRN-017** The main button sits in the bottom-right corner, arrow-shaped, 112 dp wide whatever quick actions show beside it (TRN-016; under them in the desktop action area, two thirds of the row beside Undo, WEB-018), with an icon above the words of what a tap does (TRN-063). Tap = what it says; hold = End turn, with a small haptic tick, in every response mode, except: holding Resolve (STK-029) or a Stack wait's "Assume resolves" (TRN-070) resolves the whole Stack: your phone presses Resolve at each of your priority points and Assume at each of the other player's (where the information rule allows it, TRN-071), whatever your response mode, until the Stack is empty, a decision of yours, a reveal stop (HIS-110), a greyed Assume, your own action, or the other player's real input arriving; that input is never passed over: if it reaches the server before the chain's next press, that press isn't made and you get priority with it on top (where your response mode decides, as always). Holding an Assume with nothing on the Stack ("Assume pass", "Assume no blockers", "Assume blockers") is its tap; holding a greyed one says why. (r74)
- **TRN-063** The main button holds whatever goes on now. It tells an assumption from your own action at a glance: your own actions (every name below, and Cancel request, TRN-004) are white with a grey outline and dark teal words; an assumption, the wait's Assume (TRN-070), is filled with the Assume violet (the Assume setting's, TRN-059) with white words under the Assume setting's icon (⏩); greyed is greyed whichever it is. It says under an icon what a tap does, with room for two lines (wrapped between words; drawn smaller rather than cut where two lines don't fit), an icon per kind: "To combat" (crossed swords), "End turn" (a turn arrow; not stopping at your own main phases, TRN-057; in your own second main phase only in Normal and Quick, which don't stop at your end step), "To end step" (a moon; in Control, in your own second main phase: a tap goes on to your end step, where Control stops; at your end step it reads "End turn"), a pass with the Stack empty naming where it goes, the next step in turn order: "To upkeep", "To draw step", "To Main 1", "To attackers", "To blockers" ("To end of combat" when nothing attacks), "To damage", "To end of combat", "To Main 2", "To end step", "To next turn" (an arrow in your own turn, a skip mark in the other player's), "Attack (2)" (crossed swords) / "No attack" (a struck circle), "Block (1)" (a shield) / "No blocks" (a struck shield), "Resolve" (a play mark) with something on the Stack (STK-029), and "OK" (a tick) while your division of combat damage is open (CMB-033); while the other player must act it holds the wait's Assume (TRN-070). While a panel or bar of yours is open, its go-on action is the main button instead of a button of its own, saying what it does: the cast panel's "Cast" ("Activate", "Play", STK-052), a target bar's "Done" or "None" (STK-047), a choice's "OK" (the legend rule's "Keep", STK-030), "Pay" (STK-049), the Keep / Bottom screen's "OK" (TRN-062) and the reveal panel's "OK" (HIS-110); it is greyed while that can't be done yet (a tap shakes it and holding it says why, "Finish the choice first."). Panels that keep their own buttons: a choice answered by the button tapped (Yes / No, a player, a colour, an option, "Don't pay"), the game's result dialog (TRN-065: it also shows over the lobby, where there is no main button) and the dialogs over the screen (undo requests, rewinds, conceding, choosing for the other player). Nothing sits under the Stack. It is greyed when it can't act (your cast or another choice open with nothing for it to do, such as a single target to tap, the opening, a declaration Automatum doesn't allow, a division not yet complete): a tap shakes it and holding it says why. A second tap within 0.3 s of a press it took is ignored, whatever the button says by then (a quick double tap on Cast casts once and never also passes). When the result of your own action (a tap or hold of the main button, or a card tap that makes an action) hasn't shown 0.15 s after the press, a soft violet band sweeps across the main button with a thin violet line along its bottom (white over an Assume) until it shows, at least 0.3 s; a quicker result looks as before, and playback never shows it. When the move passes to you it pulses once in your colour (TRN-075). It never shows a press your phone is about to make itself: while a play shown or a combat's hold (HIS-112, HIS-113) only holds back a step your phone then makes by itself (your pass by your response mode, an End turn or a Resolve chain going on), it holds nothing meanwhile instead of flashing "Resolve", and after that step it shows what you actually have to press; likewise on your own action's board shown at once (SYS-025) when your phone passes for you right after it by itself (your own cast in Normal or Quick), until that pass is made. (r76)
- **TRN-023** Your opening's Mulligan and Keep (TRN-062) are each one Undo step, however many changes they make; the settings in the quick actions (TRN-059) are no events at all (SYS-071). (r60)
- **TRN-059** The quick-actions row holds your own settings for this game, on either player's turn. In live play: the response mode (a tap goes on to the next of Control, Normal and Quick; its name shows), "Assume resolves" (a tap goes on to the next of Never, Normal, When they have no mana and Always, HIS-096; it reads "Assume: Never", "Assume: Normal", "Assume: No mana" or "Assume: Always") and "Autotap mana". The response mode and Assume resolves cells always fill by how much control you keep: white words and icon on deep blue for Control / Assume: Never, violet for Normal / Assume: Normal, amber for Quick / Assume: No mana, red-orange for Assume: Always. In catch-up playback (HIS-094) the same three cells in the same places: the response mode as live play's (its name, colour and tap; playback stops where it stops, HIS-095), "Autotap mana", and Assume resolves greyed (your phone assumes nothing while you catch up; a tap shakes it, holding it says why), so the row never changes between two and three cells. Equal-width cells with an icon and words; a toggle on is filled violet with a check, off white with an empty ring; holding one says what it does. Only you see or change them (the other player's phone learns your response mode, STK-036, and your "Assume resolves", never shown on their screen: so it shows its own player's move only where your phone really waits for them, TRN-058). Other quick actions: your opening's (TRN-062) and combat staging's (CMB-048, CMB-034). (r57)

### Game start and mulligans

- **TRN-027** A game begins with both players "Mulliganing" at the same time (TRN-062); keeping makes you Ready. Each player's mulligans are their own: every declaration is taken as soon as it is made, in the order the players make them, not in rounds. While both openings are open, each player's own mulligan is their move (LOB-022, LOB-072). Once the starting player has kept, they may press "Assume keep" (TRN-070, HIS-096) and play turn 1 at once while the other player still decides; each opening move of the other player's after that makes the turn again on top of it, standing as far as it comes out the same (HIS-115, HIS-116), and the other player sees none of it before keeping (HIS-117). (r76)
- **TRN-029** There is no Turn 0: both players mulligan during turn 1, while their opening status is set. (r25)
- **TRN-062** The opening follows the game's rules (opening hand 7, the first mulligan free, London mulligans, CR 103.5), both players declaring at once. While you are "Mulliganing" your quick actions are "Mulligan" (taken at once, without waiting for the other player's declaration: your hand goes back, your library is shuffled and seven are drawn; from the second mulligan on, the Keep / Bottom screen, STK-043, then opens at once for the cards it puts on the bottom, one more each time, and Mulligan and Keep are greyed until its OK, the main button (TRN-063), "Put the card on the bottom first.") and "Keep" (keeps the hand as it is); nothing else asks about it or covers the board. (r76)

### Life

- **TRN-030** Each player starts at 20 life. Tapping a life square opens a floating Life panel with that player's life (only the rules change it) and, for the other player, their presence line (TRN-043), for you the quick messages and Write (LOB-139); its life row sits on the panel edge facing the square (the panel opens under the opponent's square and above your own, in landscape too). (r59)
- **TRN-034** Portrait: each player's row shows the life square and packed piles, the other player's also their hand's fan (ZON-001); the room beside them stays free. Your row has no margin above your life square and no room under it. (r64)
- **TRN-035** Landscape (phone layout): each player's life square and piles sit in their bar row (TBL-009), the opponent's in the top bar and yours in the bottom bar, their emblems right after them. (r57)

### Emblems

- **TRN-053** A player's emblems show as small round chips (24 dp) with a violet edge in their row of round chips by the life square, after their dungeon's room and their states (TRN-069, TRN-072), oldest first: each with the emblem's art from the card records (the emblem printing with the same rules text), or an emblem icon when there is none; beyond three, the fourth chip reads "+N" for the rest. The row's chips are all one size and never overlap. In portrait the row sits at the right of the free room right of the player's piles, inside the player's row and clear of the hands on any width, over two lines when one isn't enough (that room is always at least 96 dp while there are chips: where the piles don't leave it, they scroll under it); in landscape right after the player's piles in their bar row (TBL-009), over two lines only where the bar is tall enough, clear of the hands and of your quick actions (your row gives way to your hand in the bottom bar, TBL-009). Where the chips don't fit, they shrink (to 20 dp at least) and then fewer show: the states' last chip reads "+N" for the states left out, and the emblems' last "+N" for theirs, the emblems keeping at least half the chips. A tap on one does nothing; holding any of them inspects all that player's emblems (TBL-104) until the finger lifts: one dark panel each, outlined in its owner's colour, with the emblem's art (or the icon), "Emblem" and its full text. (r62)
- **TRN-072** What the rules keep for a player and show nowhere else shows as round chips like the emblems' (TRN-053: the same size and violet edge, a pale violet fill), in the same row, before the emblems: Monarch, Initiative, City's blessing, Enduring story, speed, the Ring's level, the player's counters (poison, energy, …), dungeons completed, extra turns and skipped turns. Each chip shows its state's own icon in dark violet, with its current level under it where it has one: a crown for Monarch, a compass for Initiative, a castle for City's blessing, an open book for an enduring story, a speedometer with the speed, a ring with the Ring's level (of 4), a drop with the poison, a bolt with the energy, stars with the experience, the radiation sign with the rad counters, a trophy with the dungeons completed, a redo arrow with the extra turns and a skip arrow with the turns skipped; any other player counter shows its first letter and count, or its first two letters where an earlier counter's chip shows that letter ("S1" shield, then "St2"). They stay in the player's row on any width, never over the hand: where they don't all fit, the last reads "+N" (TRN-053). A tap does nothing; holding one shows what it means in the dark rule popup (TBL-132, placed as SYS-045), with its words first where they say more than its name ("Speed 2", "Ring 2/4"), a "+N" what all it stands for, until the finger lifts. Both players see the same. (r74)
- **TRN-073** Day or night (CR 731) belongs to the game: a round chip like TRN-072's, a sun for day or a moon for night, right-aligned just above the Stack, in both orientations, while it is one of them. Holding it says what it means. (r62)
- **TRN-069** A player's dungeon (LOB-117) shows as a small card in a fourth place after Library, Graveyard and Exile (in landscape too, in the bar row, TBL-009), and the room its marker is on as a round chip with a door, first in the player's row of round chips (TRN-072), whose hold names it ("Room: <room>"). Tapping the dungeon does nothing, holding inspects it, and it can't be dragged. Dungeons to choose from show as cards; a room's ability on the Stack shows its dungeon. (r62)

### Opponent presence

- **TRN-042** The opponent's life square has a small dot in its corner: green when they have this game open and touched it in the last 2 minutes, yellow when open but idle, grey when away. It is hidden while you are disconnected. (r29)
- **TRN-043** The opponent's Life panel has an info line with the same dot: "Online", "Idle 7 min", "Last seen 12 min ago", "Last seen 3 h ago", "Last seen Oct 27" or "Not seen yet". (r29)

### Waiting, assumptions and results

- **TRN-046** Suggest game end opens a chooser My win / Draw game, then a confirm dialog ("Suggest my win?" or "Suggest draw?") with an optional one-line "Reason", at most 200 characters (placeholder "Infinite combo"); both players see the reason in quotes in the suggestion panel (TRN-047). (r45)
- **TRN-047** A waiting suggestion shows as a panel on the table, not a dialog: "<name> proposed their win." (or "a draw"; "You proposed your win." for the author). The opponent gets Decline and Accept, greyed for its first 2 seconds as an ending's OK is (TRN-065; an agreed result, which ends the game at once: the player accepting waits at the table for the author's OK on the result dialog, TRN-065), the author Withdraw; made at the newest event. (r60)
- **TRN-064** Concede (a flag icon) in Table actions asks "Concede?" ("You lose this game at once.", Cancel / Concede in red); Concede ends the game at once as your loss (CR 104.3a), also while a suggestion waits: you wait at the table for the other player's OK on the result dialog (TRN-065). If the other player's latest actions haven't reached you yet, the game first branches at your local head, so the result never depends on what you haven't seen. (r48)
- **TRN-065** Every ending (a loss by the rules, a draw, the loop draw of TRN-060, a concession, TRN-064, an accepted suggestion, TRN-047) shows each player a dialog in the middle of the screen once the end is shown to them: big "You won", "You lost" or "Draw", under it how it ended ("Bob's life reached 0", "You drew from an empty library", "Bob got 10 poison counters", "<card>'s effect", "Bob conceded", "You conceded", "Agreed: <reason>" ("Agreed" without one), "Both players lost at once", "An endless loop"), the eye at its bottom left (hides the dialog to look at what is behind it; only the eye stays, at the same spot) and OK at its right. OK always works, but is greyed for the dialog's first 2 seconds, so a misplaced tap can't accept it. A result the rules gave is final only with both players' OKs, whatever the other player has seen: after your OK you stay at the table, the dialog says "Waiting for <name> to acknowledge" and Take back OK takes OK's place, working until their OK comes; it can't be declined, and Undo (HIS-084) takes the step back until it is final, with the usual approval, and every OK goes with it. Both OKs make it final and recorded (LOB-126), the player who pressed first is notified (LOB-111), and only then does each go to the lobby (LOB-046). The game waits for whoever hasn't pressed theirs (TRN-058), with reminders (LOB-075, LOB-076); nothing is recorded until they do, and a player who never does leaves it to the cleanup (LOB-133). Your OK and its take-back show on your other devices and to the other player at once, and an OK is never played back. A concession or an accepted suggestion is final and recorded at once and can't be undone; the player whose own action ended the game (the conceder, the player who accepted the suggestion) has given their OK with it. Whatever the ending, nobody goes to the lobby until both players have pressed OK: after a concession or an accepted suggestion the player whose action ended it stays at the table with the dialog saying "Waiting for <name> to acknowledge" (no button), and the other player stays with the dialog until they tap OK (whenever and on whichever device they open the game), never closed for them in the background; both then go to the lobby. Either can leave for other screens as usual; once the game was closed, opening it shows the same dialog over the lobby, which takes nothing until both OKs stand (its Cancel game is gone; behind the dialog, the status line says "<name> hasn't seen the last game's result yet" and Ready is greyed, "Tap OK on the last game's result first."), and the server refuses every lobby action of that game meanwhile (decks, Ready, starting player, Show deck, settings, Cancel game: "Both players tap OK on the last game's result first."). The next game starts only once both have pressed OK (the server keeps each player's OK). (r49)
- **TRN-066** The wait: while the other player must act (online or away), a semi-transparent dark band right under their hand (in portrait under their row, which holds it, ZON-001; on the desktop table at the battlefields' top, from their seat header to the battlefields' right edge, WEB-016), edge to edge and fading out toward both sides only over a narrow margin, every line of its words on its solid middle (in portrait and landscape alike), in white text of up to 3 lines (never taking a touch; in landscape too; not while a play is shown, HIS-112), says why: "<name> may respond to <spell>" (an ability as "<source>'s ability", STK-046), "<name> may act · <step>", "<name> is declaring blockers", "<name> is deciding on a mulligan", "<name> is choosing · <card>" (the card only when you can see it), "Waiting for <name>", which, when they are behind you (you went ahead on assumptions about them they haven't confirmed), says where they are instead: "Bob is at turn 5 · their combat" (a combat step as "combat", any other step by its name, "your" in your own turn). (r64)
- **TRN-070** Where the other player's answer may be assumed (HIS-096), the main button holds "Assume resolves" (something on the Stack, STK-028), "Assume pass" (nothing on the Stack, as after their blocks), "Assume no blockers" / "Assume blockers" (CMB-040), or "Assume keep" (their opening, once your own opening is done, TRN-027), each in the Assume violet with the Assume icon (TRN-063), while they must act, whether they are online or away, with no timeout, also at points they began, where only their mode decides whether your phone waits (HIS-096); holding "Assume resolves" resolves the whole Stack (TRN-017), holding any other Assume is its tap; greyed where assuming would show you hidden information or a random result (TRN-071). Unless "Assume resolves" is Never, their pause at your end step and right after you attack is never a wait (HIS-096). A tap of Assume and your phone's own assumption are the same press (HIS-109). Your phone never assumes the other player's keep by itself: only a press of Assume keep does. (r76)
- **TRN-067** Information rule: while your line rests on an unconfirmed assumption, nothing you do or assume may show you a card you couldn't see (the other player's hand or library, face-down cards, random discards; never your own library) or a random result (coin, die, random choice), apart from what the game's settings allow (LOB-135: by default the other player's library cards, not their hand's; with "Allow assumes that reveal hands" their hand cards too, a random discard of theirs included; never face-down cards or other random results). - **TRN-071** Under the information rule (TRN-067, with the game's settings LOB-135: a reveal they allow neither stops nor greys anything), your phone's own passes after an action of yours stop before one that would show you something (you keep priority there), and such an assumption isn't made: the wait reads "Waiting for <name> · going on would show you hidden cards or a random result" (TRN-066) and the main button holds the wait's Assume ("Assume resolves", "Assume pass") greyed: a tap shakes it and holding it says "Assuming this would show you hidden cards or a random result: wait for <name>." (r74)
- **TRN-068** The unconfirmed mark: while actions of yours rest on answers the other player hasn't confirmed yet, a small violet dot (no number) sits at the main button's corner; a tap or hold says "<name> hasn't seen your last moves yet; if <name> answers differently, they'll be taken back". Only you see it. (r50)
- **TRN-048** Decline and Withdraw are their own events, not undos. (r18)
- **TRN-049** While a suggestion waits, every other game action is blocked for a player once the board shown to them includes it; chat, timeline browsing and holding to inspect still work. (r48)
- **TRN-050** A suggestion left behind on a discarded line (an earlier real answer of the other player's branched, HIS-097) no longer locks anything. (r48)
- **TRN-051** The suggestion panel appears once the board shown to you includes its event (in catch-up playback when it is reached, TRN-049), and its buttons work from then on. (r60)

### Priority and resolution

- **STK-026** Priority is passed by itself for a player who could only pass, judged on public information (no cards in hand, nothing else hidden they could use), by whichever device runs the step; never in their own main phases with an empty Stack. Your device also passes for you where your response mode doesn't stop (STK-036), and a caster passes after a cast. When both players pass in a row the top of the Stack resolves, and play goes on by itself to the next point where someone can act. (r48)
- **STK-036** Your response mode (TRN-059) sets where your device stops for you, in live play and in catch-up playback alike (HIS-095): Control at every point where you get priority (after your own cast or activation you keep it; in your own turn also at the beginning of combat, CMB-047, and your end step, which the main button in your second main phase taps on to, TRN-063); Normal at your stops (your own main phases with an empty Stack) and pauses (the other player's spell or ability on top of the Stack, their attack, blocks, their end step); Quick (the default) as Normal, but passing at pauses where you can't respond and answering one-answer questions (SYS-063), never the order of your triggers (STK-030) or cards picked up to an amount (STK-061). A new mode applies from the next point on: the stop you stand at when you change it, live or in playback, stays until you answer it (never passed by itself; where the other player's phone then assumes your pass, your table stops at that assumption). It is a value of each game kept on the server, outside undo and history: the other player's phone learns it at once when you change it (never shown on their screen) and assumes your pass wherever your mode would pass; at points you began only your mode decides there, so it waits where your mode stops (HIS-096). Each event records the mode it was made under (an assumption: the mode of the player it is about). (r58)
- **STK-053** In every response mode your own main phases are never passed for you (casting or playing a land there leaves you there with priority), and choices and declarations of attackers and blockers always wait for their player. (r52)
- **STK-037** At your stop or pause the table is yours to act on, with no time limit and no Hold: the main button (TRN-063), reading Resolve with something on the Stack (STK-029). (r53)
- **STK-044** In the phone layout (the desktop table: WEB-019) every Automatum panel (open choices, target bars, the reveal panel, HIS-110) sits where the cast panel does (a damage division is attached to the combat band instead, CMB-033): the table's full width (in landscape at most 420 dp from its left edge), white with a violet top edge, its bottom on the top of your hand row so your hand is never covered. Each has the eye, at the left of its bottom row or slim bar, which hides it to show the board (only the eye stays) and brings it back (cards picked by tapping stay picked, as in a search, STK-056). No Hold button. Titles say only the choice ("Scry 2", "Choose any target", "Pay {R}" with its symbols), except the cast panel's, which names its card (STK-023), and the reveal panel's, which names who revealed and the source (HIS-110), and the sentence the question asks about shows under them (STK-030); what the choice comes from is otherwise never named in text but sits at the panel's or bar's right, in the same place in each (a damage division's creature at its top left instead, CMB-033), as a small card: a spell or permanent as its card, an ability as its Stack ability token (STK-046, also when its source has left, as a land sacrificed to activate it); holding it inspects it (TBL-104). A panel with no source (a discard to hand size, the legend rule) has none. A game's result is a dialog in the middle of the screen instead (TRN-065). Every card in a panel (the source too) is the hand's 28×40 dp times the Panels card size (TBL-034), except the reveal panel's revealed cards, at the Hand card size (HIS-110); a card a tap or drop moves within a panel (scry, surveil, putting cards in order) slides to its new place while the others slide to close the gap, at the card move speed (TBL-038); holding a panel card to inspect it moves nothing in the panel. (r58)
- **STK-028** The other player sees the wait "Bob may respond to Storm Spark" (TRN-066) with "Assume resolves" on the main button where it may be assumed (a tap assumes Bob lets it resolve), and no Pass for Bob (TRN-015). (r54)
- **STK-029** While a spell or ability is on the Stack and you hold priority, the main button (TRN-063) reads "Resolve" (a play icon above the word): a tap passes your own priority; holding it resolves the whole Stack (TRN-017). Nothing sits under the Stack. While your cast or a choice is open, or a reveal panel (HIS-110), it holds their go-on action instead, or is greyed (TRN-063). Not while your phone is about to pass by itself: once a play shown is done, or right after your own cast (TRN-063). (r76)
- **STK-030** A choice Automatum meets while resolving or putting triggers on the Stack ("you may …", choosing cards, a trigger's targets and modes, the order of triggers, which replacement applies first, the legend rule, a payment) belongs to the player the rules name, who answers it in a panel above the hand (STK-044): a title saying only the choice ("Scry 2"), the source at the panel's right (STK-044), "About:" with the cards it concerns, a short grey instruction and the choice, cards drawn as cards. Without holding anything each question shows the sentence it asks about under its title: an ability's whole text (a keyword's trigger as its keyword's printed line; a trigger's targets picked on the table as the target bar shows them, STK-047, also when its source has left the battlefield or can't be seen, the ability's text being public), a resolving spell's sentence naming what is chosen; a "you may" is its own sentence with what follows it ("You may pay" over "You may forage. If you do, put two +1/+1 counters on it."), a cost to pay a "Cost" chip with its mana and tap symbols ("{2}", "{T}", "Forage"), and a cost of two ways asks which with what it is ("To forage, exile three cards from your graveyard or sacrifice a Food.", Exile / Sacrifice). Triggers to order and replacement effects to order are drag-to-reorder lists of full-width rows (a handle at the left, the place number, the card with its whole line; a trigger whose source has left the battlefield, a creature that died, shows that source and its line as it was there, CR 608.2h), opening in a suggested order (triggers the engine's, the top one resolving first; replacements the engine's), then OK; dragging a handle moves its row. Replacements are a preference list (CR 616.1): they apply in its order, skipping ones no longer applicable, and you are asked again only when a new one appears. When two or more of your triggers go on the Stack at the same time you are always asked their order, in every response mode (CR 603.3b); triggers that are exactly the same (one ability of one source triggering twice) are put on without asking. A number to choose has the same − value + with the numeric input as an X (STK-023); a player to choose is a button per player with their round avatar and name; the legend rule shows every permanent with the name to tap the one to keep, then Keep. A choice's OK (the legend rule's Keep) is the main button (TRN-063). A choice of permanents on the battlefield (an edict's sacrifice, "choose a creature", "choose a creature an opponent controls") is picked on the table instead (STK-047), as is a payment (STK-049). Nobody chooses for them (TRN-066). While the same choice stays open the panel stays, and what you have picked in it, whatever else arrives meanwhile (the other player's result suggestion or undo request). The cards a choice offers its own player show face up when they are theirs to see, also a card the same step put in their hand just before asking (connive's drawn card in its discard choice, problem report JN5UQN). (r62)
- **STK-056** A search (a fetch land's "Search your library for a basic land card, …", a trigger's "you may search your library for …") is such a choice: its title says what is searched, from "Search" up to the next comma ("Search your library for a basic land card"), its source at the right (STK-044); the cards it may find are picked by tapping, then OK; under them, "Other cards" shows the searched zone's other cards (in a search of the other player's library, theirs), dimmed and sorted by name: tapping one does nothing, holding one inspects it. (r54)
- **STK-061** Collecting evidence as something resolves is one such choice (STK-030): after "you may", the cards of your graveyard are picked by tapping in one panel, as many as you like; its instruction says the amount ("Tap cards with total mana value 8 or more, then OK.", as casting with collect evidence and crew's does with total power), OK is greyed until their mana values reach it and stays enabled for more cards, and those you picked are exiled together. It is asked even when only all your cards reach the amount, in every response mode. (r70)
- **STK-060** When several of your permanents explore at once, you choose which goes next (CR 701.44d) in a panel of their cards; one that has already left the battlefield is shown by what it was (hidden only if it was face down), since it still explores. When all of them have left you aren't asked: the order changes nothing, and each still reveals in turn. (r70)
- **STK-043** A choice between two places is one screen of two labelled areas with a count ("Top · 2", "Bottom · 1"): scry (Top / Bottom), surveil (Top / Graveyard), separating piles (Pile 1 / Pile 2, or Face-up / Face-down pile), discarding (Hand / Discard), bottoming (Keep / Bottom). Cards start in the first area; a tap moves a card to the end of the other area and back, a drag drops it where released. (r42)
- **STK-054** In a two-area choice (STK-043) cards whose order matters (scry's areas, surveil's Top) are numbered, 1 nearest the top; each area has an "All to …" icon button; OK is enabled once the count fits (exactly one for "discard a card") and sends choice and order in one step. Ordering alone ("put them back in any order") is the same screen with one area. (r42)
- **STK-041** Naming a card ("choose a card name", CR 201.4) is a search field autocompleting card names, never free text: the names containing what was typed, first those of your own library as your phone knows them (a card-stack icon), then those of cards you see this game (public zones and your own hand, an eye icon), then every card of the device's card records (from two letters on), each name once and each passing the effect's restriction ("a nonland card name"). A card with several names offers each by itself and never both together, judged by that part alone: either half of a split card, either side of a double-faced card, a flip card's, Adventure's, Omen's or prepare spell's other name; a tap on one chooses it (written in the field) and OK, greyed until a name is chosen or one offered is written, names it. The other player's hidden cards never show or change the order. Choosing a creature type is a list of every creature type of the rules with a field narrowing it; a tap chooses one. Neither lists the other player's hidden cards or their types. (r57)
- **STK-062** A copy of the card with a chosen name ("create a copy of the card with the chosen name", CR 707.12) is made at once from the card records of the phone of the player who makes it, the card built as a deck's cards are; nothing is asked. Without card records the game waits there.
- **STK-032** While Automatum's choice is open, casting and every action that changes what Automatum works with are refused on both devices: what it was made on shakes (TBL-118), on the other player's device their "is choosing" text flashes (HIS-075), and holding a greyed menu row or the main button says "Automatum is waiting for a choice. Answer it first." Looking, inspecting and Undo still work. (r53)
