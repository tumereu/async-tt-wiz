# Web client

The app in a browser, served by the ATW server beside its API (one address for both). Every other entry applies to the
web client too unless it is marked "(Android)" or an entry here says otherwise. The phone layout is the Android app in a
browser; the desktop layout arranges the same parts for a large window, a mouse and a keyboard (WEB-013, WEB-015).

### Addresses and Back

- **WEB-001** Every screen has its own address: `/` the menu, `/games`, `/games/<id>` (a game), `/pods`, `/pods/<id>/<tab>` (draft, build, stats or games), `/decks`, `/decks/import`, `/decks/<id>`, `/decks/<id>/edit`, `/cubes/new`, `/cubes/<id>`, `/contacts`, `/settings` and `/stats`. Refreshing a page opens the same screen, and an address opened while signed out opens once you have signed in; an address of no screen opens the menu. (r71)
- **WEB-002** The browser's Back and Forward move between screens, never into dialogs or menus. Opening a screen adds a history entry; returning to the screen you came from (an on-screen Back) goes back an entry instead of adding one, so Back never loops; switching a pod's tabs keeps one entry. Back from a game goes straight to the screen before it, without SYS-050's second Back. (r71)
- **WEB-003** Every screen but the menu has a back arrow at the left of its header: it does what Android's Back does there (closes an open panel or the side menu first, steps back inside a section, such as Games → Choose opponent), and on a screen with nothing to step back from opens the screen it belongs to (a game's Games, a section's menu). (r71)
- **WEB-004** Esc closes the open dialog, popup, panel or side menu, never a screen, except on a game's table and the draft: there Esc with nothing open leaves the screen, as Back does (a game's table also closes the chat and leaves timeline mode first). On Android Chrome the phone's Back closes them too (where the browser supports it); with none open it is the browser's Back. (r72)

### Loading and updates

- **WEB-005** The first visit shows a loading screen (the wizard hat, "ATW", "Loading Async Tabletop Wizards…" and a moving bar) until the app draws; later visits load from the browser's cache. When the app can't load it says "ATW couldn't load. Check your connection and reload the page." (r71)
- **WEB-006** A tab left open on an older version reloads itself when the server has a newer one (checked when the page opens, when it comes back to the foreground and every 10 minutes): at once on the menu, the Games list or the sign-in page, or as a game is opened; never while a dialog, panel or menu is open or text is being typed. Elsewhere it waits until you reach one of those. After the reload a short note says "ATW was updated", and What's new (LOB-087) follows its usual rules; a first visit counts as a fresh install. (r71)

### Sign-in, card records and sound

- **WEB-007** A game opens in the browser as in the app: a started game's table with the hand, panels, combat band, timeline and chat, also from its address (a refresh opens it again); games still being set up open their lobby, and pods their draft, build, stats and games. Its Back leaves the table at once, without SYS-050's second Back. The game itself (your actions and their outcomes, passes, End turn, the other player's moves), what you can play from your hand and with your cards, a raised hand card's cost chips, and the steps of a cast, of a choice or payment while something resolves and of combat (what may attack or block, its costs and damage) are worked out beside the table, so it stays responsive meanwhile and the hand, menus and panels show them as they arrive. (r72)
- **WEB-008** The "Your move" sound (LOB-146, TRN-075) plays in the browser, also in a background tab, once you have tapped or typed on the page after opening it (browsers allow sound only then). (r71)
- **WEB-009** The card records (LOB-091) download into the browser's own storage, which is asked to keep them (persistent storage), and are used from there on later visits; if the browser deletes them (iOS Safari does after 7 days unvisited unless the site is installed), they download again. Sign-in is kept in the browser too, across reloads, until you log out. (r71)
- **WEB-010** "Import from link" (LOB-061) reads Archidekt decks through the ATW server, since Archidekt answers no browser directly. (r71)

### The table in the browser

- **WEB-011** On a game's table and the draft the browser's own gestures stay off: the page doesn't scroll, bounce or pull to refresh, a pinch, double tap, Ctrl+wheel or Ctrl with + or - doesn't zoom the page, and a long press or right-click shows no browser menu, callout or text selection; the table's own pan, pinch, hold and double tap get every touch. Other screens keep the browser's usual behaviour. (r72)
- **WEB-012** The table's haptic ticks (the move passing to you, TRN-075; a held quick action) vibrate the phone where the browser can (Android's Chrome), once the page has had a tap; elsewhere (iOS, desktops) there is none, and nothing fails. (r72)

### Layouts

- **WEB-013** The app draws in one of two layouts: the phone layout (the Android app as it is, portrait or landscape by the window's shape) or the desktop layout (the desktop table, WEB-015). Automatic chooses the desktop layout when the window is at least 1024 × 600 and the main pointer can hover (a mouse or trackpad), otherwise the phone layout, following the window as it changes. The Android app always draws the phone layout. (r73)
- **WEB-014** Settings → Table → "Layout" (in a browser only): Automatic (the default), Phone or Desktop, each with what it means; kept in that browser and applied at once. (r73)

### The desktop table

- **WEB-015** The desktop layout's table is the same game table arranged for a large window: a global top row (WEB-020), the battlefields and combat band between the players' seat headers (WEB-016), a right dock (WEB-017), and a bottom band with your hand centred in it and the action area at its right (WEB-018). Everything else works as on the phone's landscape table: it opens as TBL-121 does, and the combat band, plays shown, the timeline, reveals, card panels, your hand row's cards from other zones, the effects marker, counters, chips and menus are the same. (r73)
- **WEB-016** Each player's seat header lies on their own battlefield, the other player's above theirs and yours between your battlefield and your hand, in the player's colour (grey in timeline mode): their avatar and name ("You" for you), life square, Library, Graveyard and Exile, their dungeon, and their round chips (states, emblems, TRN-053, TRN-072), each working as on the phone; the other player's hand is a row of small card backs after them, with its count. The wait band (TRN-066) and the other player's Stack line (STK-057) lie under their seat header, across the battlefields. (r73)
- **WEB-017** The right dock (320 wide, from under the top row to the action area): the Stack as its full list, always open, top first as the Stack list shows it (STK-018; "Empty" with nothing on it), and the game chat under it with its input always shown (Game and Pod tabs in a pod game, LOB-145); messages showing in it are read (LOB-138). The dock button in the top row folds it away and shows it again: folded away, the battlefields take its width, the Stack is the HUD at their right edge (STK-007) and the top row has the chat button (LOB-066). (r73)
- **WEB-018** The action area at the bottom right, under the dock: the quick actions (TRN-059) in a row, and under them Undo at the left and the main button (TRN-063, 176 wide; the quiet waiting status in its place, TRN-004) at the right. (r73)
- **WEB-019** Panels are placed as in MTG Arena: a decision that needs the board (picking targets, paying with permanents, picking on the battlefield, a payment while something resolves, the waiting line) is a strip at the bottom centre of the battlefields, its bottom on your seat header; one that doesn't (the cast panel's ways, extras, X and modes, a choice while something resolves, the Keep / Bottom screen, a reveal) is a centred overlay over the battlefields, with the battlefields and the dock dimmed and taking no clicks while it shows (its eye hides it and the dimming, STK-044). Either way the go-on is the main button; a damage division stays attached to the combat band (CMB-033). (r73)
- **WEB-020** The top row holds only what belongs to the whole game: the side menu button, the turn line and move pill (TRN-005, TRN-006), the phase track (the phase dialog's phase labels over their step squares, the current step outlined in the turn player's colour and flashing on a step change; view-only, so there is no step button or phase dialog), Return to current in timeline mode, the chat button while the dock is folded away, the dock button and Table actions. The event text (HIS-075) shows at the battlefields' top, under the other player's seat header. (r73)
- **WEB-021** Timeline mode on the desktop table: the timeline strip with its gear runs along the bottom band under your hand, Previous / Auto / Next take the action area, and the seat headers turn grey (HIS-008). (r73)
- **WEB-022** Card sizes (TBL-034) are kept for each layout: the desktop layout's start larger (battlefield 1.5×, hand 2×, piles, Stack and panels 1.5×; piles still fit their seat header), and Settings changes those of the layout it is drawn in. (r73)
