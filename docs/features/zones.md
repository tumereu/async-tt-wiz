# Zones

Hand, library, graveyard and exile, the graveyard/exile view, and who sees what in each zone.

### Hand

- **ZON-001** Your hand row shows your cards face up. The other player's hand shows card backs: in portrait as a small fan of them in their life and pile row, after their piles, with its count at its right (it has no row of its own; the battlefields take that room); in landscape as their hand row in the top bar, looking just like yours (TBL-009). Holding one of its cards inspects it as a covered card (TBL-109); cards of it revealed or looked at open in a panel (HIS-110). (r64)
- **ZON-002** A hand keeps its card order: hand cards slide aside when cards come or go, and a hand is never reordered (ZON-081). (r45)
- **ZON-003** Holding a finger on an empty part of a hand previews nothing. (r28)
- **ZON-004** Double-tapping a hand card casts it through Automatum (STK-023) as a second tap on the raised card does (ZON-079): at once when it has no choice, otherwise through its cast panel; a land is played at once onto its owner's battlefield, and a trigger it sets off (landfall) is passed on by its player as after a cast (STK-026); a card Automatum doesn't allow now (any card in the other player's hand too) stays in the hand and shakes, without a message (TBL-118). (r58)
- **ZON-077** A drag is what it means to Automatum: a hand card dropped on the Battlefield or the Stack is played or cast as by a double-tap (ZON-004), one card at a time; a creature dropped in the combat area joins the declaration (CMB-028). Nothing is placed by hand (TBL-004): a drag that starts on a battlefield card pans the table unless the combat area is there to take it, and a card dropped back on its own Battlefield or into its hand returns without a shake. Any other drag (to another zone, from a pile or panel, the Stack's entry, several hand cards, a creature that can't join) is refused: the card goes back and shakes (TBL-118). (r53)
- **ZON-078** Tapping a library opens its menu (double-tapping one does nothing: nothing is drawn by hand, SYS-069). The Library menu shows what Automatum offers for the library's top card (playing or casting it from the top, as a card that lets you play lands from the top of your library allows), one row each with the card's name ("Play · Forest", "Cast · Storm Spark"), used as in a card menu (TBL-126); your own Library's menu also always has the View deck cell (ZON-093), the other player's never. When Automatum offers nothing for the other player's Library, tapping it opens no menu and it shakes (TBL-118). (r45)
- **ZON-079** Your hand has no menu: a tap raises your card a little and outlines in amber the sources Autotap would tap for its regular cast, for its smallest cost only where it can have several (ZON-080; in a stack of identical sources its topmost ones, TBL-130; none for a land or a pool-paid cast; while a chip is held, that chip's, ZON-080). A second tap casts or plays it at once when that is all it can do now (its regular cast, a greyed way beside it being no choice, asking nothing before its payment); otherwise it opens the card's cast panel (STK-023, ZON-082) and never picks a chip, the chips flashing once over the panel as it opens, a hint that a chip casts directly. Tapping elsewhere or another hand card lowers it, as do casting it and its leaving the hand; board changes refresh its chips and outlines. It moves at hand-move speed. A card you can't use now isn't raised, only shaken; the other player's hand cards ignore taps. Cards outside your hand that a permission lets you cast or play from where they are (flashback or escape in your graveyard, a foretold card in exile, the top of your library under "you may play lands from the top of your library", …) show face up at the right end of your hand row after a thin divider, each with its zone's badge (the empty pile's glyph, white in a dark disc) in its bottom-left corner, while their pile still holds them; only cards you may see, and the other player's table shows none of yours. They are hidden while no permission applies; they glow and are tapped, double-tapped and dragged as hand cards while Automatum lets you cast or play them now, and are dull and only shake otherwise. A way using the card's own permission is named by its keyword ("Flashback {R}"). (r64)
- **ZON-080** Chips in a row above a raised hand card rise and fade with it, one per complete way to cast it (even if only one): each way of the card with its optional extras decided, named for it ("Cast", "Cast with offspring", "Cast with kicker and offspring"; another face or Adventure by its name, "Flashback", "Evoke", a split card's fused halves, its special actions "Plot", "Foretell" and its abilities used from the hand "Cycling" and its kinds, "Channel", "Ninjutsu", a plain "cost: effect" one reading as on the card, its cost and then its effect), with its whole mana cost (a long name wraps onto more rows, never cut; an X as {X}): a way that can have several costs (a cost depending on the target, a mode or the like, or a search that couldn't finish) shows the smallest legal one, the lowest mana value it can pay now and the first listed on a tie, then "+" and a generic mana symbol holding a question mark ("{R} + {?}", our own notation, not real MTG); white with a violet outline: the regular "Cast" ("Play" for a land) first, each way with its extras off first; every way shown whether or not it can be done now. At most 4 chips: a card with more complete ways shows one chip "Cast…" with its cheapest cost (the cheapest it can pay now), which opens its cast panel with every choice (ZON-082). A tap on a chip casts (or uses) the card that way at once, Autotap paying (STK-024); targets, modes and X are then asked as needed (STK-023). Holding a chip outlines the sources Autotap would tap for it and shows a card-like popup with its name, cost and printed text, casting nothing. A chip Automatum shows can't be done now is greyed and does nothing (a land's "Play" too, while it can't be played); chips show prices and never deny a card it lists (TBL-117): a way it lists with no payment Autotap can find is a live chip, and the cast panel opens to pay by hand (STK-024). An extra that can be paid several times (multikicker) is paid at most once in the chips, more often in the cast panel. (r58)
- **ZON-082** A hand card's cast panel (ZON-079, the "Cast…" chip) shows its ways as chips under "Cast as" (the one cast filled violet; a greyed one does nothing) and its extras under "Extras" as amber-outlined chips with "+" before the name and a check box (kicker, buyback, offspring, gift and other optional additional costs): a tap turns an extra on or off and never casts; one that can be paid several times (multikicker) counts up per tap ("×N") with a small "−". It opens on the first way you can pay with every extra off; changing a way or an extra keeps the choices made below it that still fit and the sources you picked that still pay (STK-024). With ways or extras to choose it opens before the targets: Cast goes on to them. (r58)
- **ZON-081** Your hand row keeps every card at its full size and never reorders: a drag that starts on it and first moves sideways pans the hand, one that first moves up or down drags the card (ZON-077). A hand wider than its row pans between its ends, stretching past an end like a rubber band and springing back on release (TBL-014); a hand that fits springs back to the middle. (r44)

### Library

- **ZON-012** A library pile never shows a card face to either player (no "known card" marker). (r45)
- **ZON-093** View deck, a cell in your own Library's menu (greyed only when you have no cards in the game), opens a panel "Deck" (a sheet as the Stack list is, closed by a borderless X at its top right as the Source dialog is) with your deck as you play it: your own cards in the game (no tokens; the deck you play with, no sideboard), as pictures in the deck views' type groups (SYS-051, "Creatures · 12"; cards by name, ×N for copies) at the Panels card size (TBL-034) as every card panel's, as many per row as fit. Two chips at its top switch between "All · N" (the whole deck, the start) and "Still in library · N" (the deck without the cards you have seen leave your library: those you can see now in your hand, on the battlefield, in a graveyard or exile; a card that left face down unseen stays in it), never in library order. Holding a card inspects it. Looking only: nothing in the game changes and no event is made. (r60)

### Library order

- **ZON-023** Library order is hidden: a card put on top or bottom of a library, or looked at and left in place, keeps that place until the library is shuffled, so draws and other top-end actions take it first. Nothing on the table shows which places are known (ZON-012). (r53)
- **ZON-024** Every line of play reshuffles the unknown parts of both libraries, known places kept (HIS-042): drawing, undoing and drawing again gives a new card. (r48)
- **ZON-026** Undoing your own look, scry or reveal needs the other player's approval (an undo request, HIS-087). (r48)

### Graveyard and exile

- **ZON-030** A graveyard or exile pile shows its newest card on top; removing it shows the next one down, and cards arriving together land in order so the last one is on top (milling 3 puts the third on top). (r27)
- **ZON-031** Library, graveyard and exile piles show only their top card at single-card size, with a card count. (r45)
- **ZON-032** Holding a graveyard or exile pile previews its top card. (r8)
- **ZON-033** Tapping either player's graveyard or exile opens its search view at once, also while reviewing the timeline or while a result is pending (looking has no game effect); another tap on it closes the view, and an empty pile shakes instead. Nothing moves a pile's cards by hand. (r61)
- **ZON-035** Double-tapping a graveyard or exile pile leaves its search view open: the first tap opened it, the second does nothing more. (r61)
- **ZON-036** Graveyards and exiles are public, so looking through either player's is free: the view is personal, records no history and is never shown to the opponent. (r45)
- **ZON-092** An exile pile holding a card with counters (a suspended card's time counters, say) carries a small marker at its top-right corner, opposite its count: a violet dot ringed in white with a white centre. (r58)

### Graveyard and exile view

- **ZON-037** The view groups cards under coloured type headers with counts like "Creature (3)" (non-empty types only), types in the shared order (SYS-051), names alphabetical, copies grouped ×N. (r35)
- **ZON-038** The view closes on ✕, on a tap outside it, or when its pile empties. (r45)
- **ZON-049** The graveyard/exile view's cards sit in up to 3 columns, left to right then down; a partial last row starts at the left. (r45)
- **ZON-058** Tapping a card in the graveyard/exile view opens its card menu (TBL-116): what Automatum offers for it (a flashback cast, say) as rows, nothing else; a card Automatum offers nothing for opens no menu. Holding it inspects it (TBL-104). (r52)
- **ZON-094** A card in the exile view shows its state as a word on its chip before its counters, and as a line in Inspect (TBL-104): "Plotted", "Foretold" (also on a card exiled face down: that it is foretold is public, what it is stays hidden) or "Warped"; copies with a different state keep their own places. (r60)
- **ZON-091** A card in the exile view shows its counters, any kind, as a battlefield card does: bubbles on its art (TBL-135, a plain "3" for three time counters), sized with the card; copies with different counters keep their own places. Holding it lists them (TBL-104). (r71)
- **ZON-061** A drag on empty space in the view, a double-tap-drag anywhere in it or two fingers scroll it; there is no selection box. (r45)

### Moving cards

- **ZON-046** Cards moving between any piles (library, graveyard, exile; yours or the opponent's) fly across the table. (r45)
- **ZON-071** Leaving the battlefield untaps a card and resets its P/T changes, counters and face (back to front); face down survives only a move to exile. (r46)

### Hidden information

- **ZON-073** Library cards are hidden from everyone, hand cards are seen only by their holder, and face-up graveyard, exile, battlefield cards and Stack entries are public; face-down cards show backs everywhere. (r35)
