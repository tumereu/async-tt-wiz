# Zones

Hand, library, graveyard and exile, the graveyard/exile view, and who sees what in each zone.

### Hand

- **ZON-001** Your hand row shows your cards face up; the opponent's hand row looks just like yours but shows card backs. (r7)
- **ZON-002** A hand keeps its card order: hand cards slide aside when cards come or go, and a hand is never reordered (ZON-081). (r45)
- **ZON-003** Holding a finger on an empty part of a hand previews nothing. (r28)
- **ZON-004** Double-tapping a hand card casts it through Automatum (STK-023), as a second tap on the raised card does (ZON-079), the way its cost chips say (ZON-080): a land is played at once onto its owner's battlefield, and a trigger it sets off (landfall) is passed on by its player as after a cast (STK-026); a card Automatum doesn't allow now (any card in the other player's hand too) stays in the hand and shakes, without a message (TBL-118). (r45)
- **ZON-077** A drag is what it means to Automatum: a hand card dropped on the Battlefield or the Stack is played or cast as by a double-tap (ZON-004), one card at a time; a creature dropped in the combat area joins the declaration (CMB-028); a card moved within its own Battlefield only changes place, and one dropped back into its hand returns without a shake. Any other drag (to another zone, from a pile or panel, the Stack's entry, several hand cards, a creature that can't join) is refused: the card goes back and shakes (TBL-118). (r44)
- **ZON-078** The Library menu shows only what Automatum offers for the library's top card (playing or casting it from the top, as a card that lets you play lands from the top of your library allows), one row each with the card's name ("Play · Forest", "Cast · Storm Spark"), used as in a card menu (TBL-126); when it offers nothing, tapping the Library opens no menu and the Library shakes (TBL-118). (r45)
- **ZON-079** Your hand has no menu: a tap raises your card a little and outlines in amber the sources Autotap would tap (none for a land or a pool-paid cast); a second tap casts or plays it the way its chips say (ZON-080), like a double-tap. Tapping elsewhere or another hand card lowers it, as do casting it and its leaving the hand; board changes refresh its chips and outlines. It moves at hand-move speed. A card you can't use now isn't raised, only shaken; the other player's hand cards ignore taps. (r51)
- **ZON-080** Chips in a row above a raised hand card rise and fade with it, one per way to cast it (even if only one), each with its name and mana cost (a long name wraps onto more rows, never cut), white with a violet outline: the regular "Cast" ("Play" for a land) first and chosen (filled violet), then the card's other ways (another face or Adventure, "Flashback", "Evoke"). A tap chooses a way; tapping the chosen way casts it that way. A chip your mana can't pay now is greyed and does nothing. The outlined sources follow the choice (ZON-082). The cast then asks only for targets, modes and X (STK-023). (r54)
- **ZON-082** After the ways come the extras: amber-outlined chips with "+" before the name and a check box (kicker, buyback, offspring, gift and other optional additional costs); a tap turns one on or off and never casts; one that can be paid several times (multikicker) counts up per tap ("×N") with a small "−". Holding any chip shows a card-like popup with its name, cost and printed text (for the chosen way, the total cost with its extras). (r51)
- **ZON-081** Your hand row keeps every card at its full size and never reorders: a drag that starts on it and first moves sideways pans the hand, one that first moves up or down drags the card (ZON-077). A hand wider than its row pans between its ends, stretching past an end like a rubber band and springing back on release (TBL-014); a hand that fits springs back to the middle. (r44)

### Library

- **ZON-012** A library pile never shows a card face to either player (no "known card" marker). (r45)
- **ZON-013** Tapping a library opens its menu only when Automatum offers something for its top card (ZON-078), otherwise the library shakes; double-tapping a library does nothing (nothing is drawn by hand, SYS-069). (r45)

### Library order

- **ZON-023** Library order is hidden: a card put on top or bottom of a library, or looked at and left in place, keeps that place until the library is shuffled, so draws and other top-end actions take it first. Nothing on the table shows which places are known (ZON-012). (r53)
- **ZON-024** Every line of play reshuffles the unknown parts of both libraries, known places kept (HIS-042): drawing, undoing and drawing again gives a new card. (r48)
- **ZON-026** Undoing your own look, scry or reveal needs the other player's approval (an undo request, HIS-087). (r48)

### Graveyard and exile

- **ZON-030** A graveyard or exile pile shows its newest card on top; removing it shows the next one down, and cards arriving together land in order so the last one is on top (milling 3 puts the third on top). (r27)
- **ZON-031** Library, graveyard and exile piles show only their top card at single-card size, with a card count. (r45)
- **ZON-032** Holding a graveyard or exile pile previews its top card. (r8)
- **ZON-033** Tapping either player's graveyard or exile opens its menu, whose only button is Search (looking has no game effect); nothing moves a pile's cards by hand. (r45)
- **ZON-035** Double-tapping a graveyard or exile pile opens its search view, as does Search in the pile menu (also while reviewing or offline). (r29)
- **ZON-036** Graveyards and exiles are public, so looking through either player's is free: the view is personal, records no history and is never shown to the opponent. (r45)
- **ZON-037** The view groups cards under coloured type headers with counts like "Creature (3)" (non-empty types only), types in the shared order (SYS-051), names alphabetical, copies grouped ×N. (r35)
- **ZON-038** The view closes on ✕, on a tap outside it, or when its pile empties. (r45)

### Moving between piles

- **ZON-046** Cards moving between any piles (library, graveyard, exile; yours or the opponent's) fly across the table. (r45)

### Graveyard and exile view

- **ZON-049** The graveyard/exile view's cards sit in up to 3 columns, left to right then down; a partial last row starts at the left. (r45)
- **ZON-058** Tapping a card in the graveyard/exile view opens its card menu (TBL-116): what Automatum offers for it (a flashback cast, say) as rows, nothing else; a card Automatum offers nothing for opens no menu. Holding it inspects it (TBL-104). (r52)
- **ZON-061** A drag on empty space in the view, a double-tap-drag anywhere in it or two fingers scroll it; there is no selection box. (r45)

### Moving cards between zones

- **ZON-071** Leaving the battlefield untaps a card and resets its P/T changes, counters and face (back to front); face down survives only a move to exile. (r46)

### Hidden information

- **ZON-073** Library cards are hidden from everyone, hand cards are seen only by their holder, and face-up graveyard, exile, battlefield cards and Stack entries are public; face-down cards show backs everywhere. (r35)
