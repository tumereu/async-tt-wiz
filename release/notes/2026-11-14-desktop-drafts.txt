add: On a computer, the web version's draft and card screens work with the mouse: in a draft a click keeps a card and a right-click burns it, Enter confirms; in Build, Prepare and the deck editor a click on a card's left or right edge moves it to that side's section (the arrow keys too), search results can be dragged into a section, a right-click on a game offers its actions, and Enter presses a dialog's main button.
